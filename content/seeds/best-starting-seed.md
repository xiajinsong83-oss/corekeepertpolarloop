A good **starting seed** buys you the first hour of Core Keeper: loot close to the Core, a peaceful meadow to grab early wool and milk, and no 20-tunnel dig just to find copper. This page lists the community-verified starting seeds we trust — every entry traces back to a Reddit, Discord or press roundup source. For how seeds actually shape your world, read the [Seeds guide](/seeds/seed-guide/).

## Why we recommend these seeds

| Seed | What you get | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **400957925946** | Useful starter items near spawn | First-timers who want a frictionless opening |
| **1** | Meadow sub-biome north of the Core | Anyone who wants Moolin / Bambuck immediately |
| **186511372992** | Meadow sub-biome southeast of the Core | Fallback if seed 1 feels cramped |

None of these "cheat" the game — they just put the fixed, seed-locked features (a meadow, starter chests) on a short leash from The Core. The ring bands themselves are identical in every world; see the [Biomes database](/database/biomes/) for distances.

## Biome layout (ring bands)

Core Keeper always builds the same concentric rings; the seed only rotates where each inner and outer biome sits. A starting-seed win means the useful fixed features fall **inside the center ring**:

- **0–140 tiles — The Undergrounds**: copper, tin, wood, stone, food crops. Our three recommended seeds keep starter loot and the meadow inside this band, so you never dig through a hard wall on day one.
- **150–450 tiles — Clay Caves + Forgotten Ruins**: tin, iron, Larva Hive. The direction you hit depends on the seed; on these starts it's a normal exploration detour, not a rescue mission.
- **450–520 tiles — The Great Wall**: the progression gate.
- **520–1200+ tiles — outer biomes**: untouched for the first session.

Meadows are special: they are small, mob-free sub-biomes that can generate inside The Undergrounds. A meadow near spawn means Moolin (Meadow Milk) and Bambuck (Wool) are catchable before you've even built a Tin Pickaxe.

## Best playstyle for each seed

- **400957925946 — "clean slate" speedrun feel**. Good for new players, co-op hosts who want everyone to gear up in parallel, or achievement hunters who want a known opening.
- **1 — rancher start**. Pick this if your goal is an early wool-and-milk farm. Trap a Moolin and a Bambuck behind fence posts before you fight Glurch.
- **186511372992 — meadow alternative**. Same rancher fantasy as seed 1, different angle. Use it if you already explored seed 1 and want a fresh layout.

## Opening route (first 60 minutes)

1. **Grab the seed at world creation.** Type it in exactly as listed — leading numbers matter. The seed is locked once the world is made.
2. **Day one undergrounds.** Mine copper + tin, build the Basic Workbench, Copper Pickaxe and Furnace. On 400957925946 the reported starter loot is a short walk away — sweep it before you dig deep.
3. **Day two — the meadow.** On seeds `1` and `186511372992`, walk north (seed 1) or southeast (186511372992) from the Core until the dirt turns into flowered grass. Set up a small pen with fence posts and trap one Moolin and one Bambuck.
4. **First bosses.** Glurch spawns ~65 tiles out at a random angle — make a scanner, then gear up to Tin before you fight. Ghorm roams the underground tunnels; Hive Mother sits ~330 tiles out in the Larva Hive.
5. **Re-verify the meadow in 1.3.0.6.** Meadow placement is seed-deterministic, but the surrounding cave chunks regenerate — don't panic if the tunnel route looks different from the original report.

## FAQ

**Which seed should a brand-new player pick?** Use `400957925946` — useful starter items sit close to the Core, so the first hour is friction-free. If you want livestock immediately, pick seed `1` (meadow north of spawn) instead.

**Do these seeds still work in 1.3.0.6?** The major biome angles and fixed meadows are seed-deterministic, but cave chunks generate on demand. The seeds below were reported on the 1.0.0.0 era; re-verify the loot positions in 1.3.0.6 before relying on them.

**Can I change my seed after creating a world?** No. The seed is locked at world creation — note it down before you commit. Use the Seed Finder tool to preview before you start.
