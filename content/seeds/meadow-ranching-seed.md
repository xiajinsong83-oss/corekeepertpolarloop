Meadows are the best early-game side hustle in Core Keeper. They are the only peaceful sub-biome in The Undergrounds, and they are home to **Moolin** (Meadow Milk) and **Bambuck** (Wool) — two resources you otherwise grind for hours. The catch: meadows are rare, angular, and you have to find them before they wander off into a fight. These community-verified seeds put meadows on your doorstep.

## Why we recommend these seeds

| Seed | What you get | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **987654321000** | Multiple meadows near the Core | Ranchers who want a big wool/milk farm fast |
| **919045411111** | Meadow with the rare green Moolin variant | Completionists and collectors |

Both seeds trade raw ore density for livestock convenience — you still mine copper and tin normally. What you skip is the hour of wandering to find a single meadow.

## Biome layout (ring bands)

Meadows generate **inside The Undergrounds (0–140 tiles)** and occasionally in Azeos' Wilderness later. In a typical world you find zero or one meadow near spawn; these seeds stack the odds:

- **0–140 tiles — The Undergrounds**: your meadow(s) sit here. Look for the bright green flowered patch against the brown dirt walls — it's the one biome with no hostile mobs.
- **150–450 tiles — Clay Caves / Forgotten Ruins**: untouched until your ranch is fenced. The meadow lets you bank milk and wool while you work toward a Tin Pickaxe.
- **520–1200+ tiles — outer ring**: later meadows in Azeos' Wilderness are possible but irrelevant to an early farm.

The exact angle of the meadow rotates per seed, but with 987654321000 the cluster is close enough that a short radial sweep from the Core finds them. See the [Biomes database](/database/biomes/) for the full ring map.

## Best playstyle for each seed

- **987654321000 — "ranch start"**. Ideal if you want a self-sufficient food-and-clothing base before you take on Glurch. Breed Moolin for milk dishes, shear Bambuck for beds and early armor.
- **919045411111 — "variants collector"**. Shared on the official Core Keeper Discord trading channel (Apr 2026), this seed is reported to have a meadow with the rare **green Moolin** variant. Use it as a second world or a multiplayer host save; rare variants don't respawn, so capture and breed them immediately.

## Opening route

1. **Create the world with the exact seed.** Note that Moolin and Bambuck only spawn at world creation — there is no re-roll inside an existing world.
2. **Sweep radially from the Core.** On 987654321000, walk straight out in 4–8 directions until you spot the bright green meadow grass. Mark the entrance on your map.
3. **Build a pen before you chase animals.** Fence posts are cheap wood. Drop a pen between the Core and the meadow so you don't lose animals to wandering into hostile tunnels.
4. **Trap one Moolin and one Bambuck.** Lead them into the pen by walking backward. You need at least one of each to start breeding — baby animals follow the adults.
5. **Breed for variants.** On 919045411111, focus on catching the green Moolin first. Variant genetics pass through breeding, so a single green Moolin plus normal partners propagates the trait.
6. **Ship the surplus.** Meadow Milk sells well and cooks into early food buffs; Wool feeds your bed / armor production.

## FAQ

**What is a Meadow in Core Keeper?** A small, mob-free sub-biome with flowered grass where Moolin (Meadow Milk) and Bambuck (Wool) spawn. They spawn at world creation and do not respawn, so you must capture them early.

**Which seed gives the most meadows?** `987654321000` — the community's reported cluster seed, with several meadows near the Core. For the rare green Moolin variant, the Discord-shared seed is `919045411111`.

**Do meadows stay the same across game versions?** Meadow placement is seed-deterministic, but the surrounding cave chunks regenerate on demand. A meadow reported on 1.0.0.0 should still be in the same angle in 1.3.0.6 — re-verify coordinates.
