Once you've opened the Great Wall, the two things that slow you down are (a) walking between distant biomes and (b) hunting down the last collectibles. These two community-verified seeds attack both: one puts an Ancient Waypoint close enough to build a fast-travel loop early, the other points at a Sunken Sea sewer scene holding the rare **Wisdom Oracle Card**.

## Why we recommend these seeds

| Seed | What you get | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **164131586535** | Early Ancient Waypoint (reported "glitch" farm) | Players who hate walking |
| **582489510745** | Sunken Sea sewer scene with the Wisdom Oracle Card | Oracle Card completionists |

These are not "better loot" seeds — they're convenience and completion seeds. Pair them with a solid [starting seed](/seeds/best-starting-seed/) on a different world if you want both.

## Biome layout (ring bands)

- **164131586535 — inner ring.** The reported waypoint sits close enough to the opening that you can charge it before you cross the Great Wall. Once two waypoints are charged (one at the Core, one out in the inner ring), the rest of your expansion is a teleport away. See the [Biomes database](/database/biomes/) for ring distances.
- **582489510745 — outer ring (Sunken Sea).** The Wisdom Card is at coords **-805, 37**, which places it in the Sunken Sea — the water-filled outer biome at 520–1200 tiles. This is a late-game seed: you need gold-tier gear and Sunken Sea access before the coordinates matter.

## Best playstyle for each seed

- **164131586535 — explorer / co-op host.** Great for any run where you'd rather spend hours building than walking. Drop a charged waypoint at your base, mine the one the seed gives you, and you've got a two-node network before Azeos.
- **582489510745 — collector.** Roll this world once you're in the outer ring, sail the Sunken Sea, and head for -805, 37. Bring diving gear, a weapon, and a waypoint charge for the trip back.

## Opening route

### For 164131586535 (waypoint seed)

1. Create the world with the exact seed.
2. Explore The Undergrounds normally — look for the blue circular Ancient Waypoint disc embedded in the ground.
3. Charge it, then mine it with high mining damage and place it at your main base (10-minute cooldown before it's usable).
4. Charge the default waypoint in front of The Core. You now have a two-node fast-travel loop.
5. Add waypoints as you unlock Clay Caves, the Great Wall gates and the outer biomes.

### For 582489510745 (Wisdom Card seed)

1. This is a **late-game** destination — get to the Sunken Sea first (520+ tiles).
2. Open the coordinate view and sail / dig toward **-805, 37**.
3. Look for a Sunken Sea **city** (metropolis) — the sewer scene lives inside a large one.
4. Clear the sewer room; the Wisdom Oracle Card is on site.
5. Note the Reddit thread's own warning: only **large** metropolis layouts are seed-locked. If the coords land on empty water, you missed the city edge — scan in a wider circle.

## FAQ

**Is the glitched waypoint seed still useful in 1.3.0.6?** The seed's waypoint placement is still deterministic, but the reported "glitch" behavior may have been patched. Use it as a normal early-waypoint seed — don't rely on an exploit that no longer exists.

**Will the Wisdom Oracle Card seed always have the sewer scene?** Only large-metropolis dungeon layouts are seed-locked. The Reddit reporter shared coords -805, 37 in a Sunken Sea city sewer; small or exploration-generated scenes may differ even on the same seed.

**What does an Ancient Waypoint do?** A charged waypoint lets you fast-travel to any other charged waypoint or portal. You can mine the ones out in the world and re-place them, with a 10-minute cooldown on the new placement.
