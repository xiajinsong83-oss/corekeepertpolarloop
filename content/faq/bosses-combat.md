faq:
  - q: "What order should I fight the bosses?"
    a: "The main bosses follow a set progression: Glurch the Abominable Mass, then Ghorm the Devourer, the Hive Mother, and Malugaz the Corrupted. Each gates the next tier of gear and world access."
  - q: "Which drops are guaranteed from bosses?"
    a: "Each main boss drops a guaranteed progression item — Glurch's Eye, Ghorm's Horn and similar key items. Beyond those, drops fall into rarity tiers (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra Rare) with the calculator showing your odds."
  - q: "How do I get boss chests?"
    a: "Some bosses spawn a chest with their loot — for example the Hive Mother's Larva Hive Chest. Check the Boss Drop Finder to see which bosses have chest loot to farm alongside direct drops."
  - q: "Can I farm bosses for rare drops?"
    a: "Yes — bosses respawn and can be re-fought. For a 1% rare drop you'd need roughly 300 kills for a 95% chance, so use the Drop Chance Calculator to set realistic expectations."
  - q: "What gear should I bring to each boss?"
    a: "Progress your gear tier alongside the boss order — copper tools for the early bosses, then iron, gold and beyond as each boss unlocks new materials. The Boss Loadout Generator suggests loadouts per fight."
  - q: "What do I do after Malugaz?"
    a: "After the main progression bosses, the world opens up — explore the Desert of Beginnings, Sunken Sea, Crystal and other biomes, fight optional bosses and build out your base."
