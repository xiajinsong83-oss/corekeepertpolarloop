faq:
  - q: "What game version is this site based on?"
    a: "The site data baseline is Core Keeper version 1.3.0.6. Patch notes are tracked in the Patches section and news is updated regularly from official sources."
  - q: "Does Core Keeper support other languages?"
    a: "Yes — the game has multiple language options, and this site publishes dedicated sections in Chinese (简体中文), Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) and Korean (한국어) in addition to English."
  - q: "Where are my saves stored?"
    a: "Core Keeper saves are stored locally on your machine in the game's save folder. When you host a co-op world, the host's save is the world file — back it up before major updates or mod changes."
  - q: "How do world seeds work?"
    a: "Each world is generated from a seed — the same seed produces the same world layout, including starting biomes and boss positions. Use the Seed Finder to browse interesting community seeds."
  - q: "What are the seasonal events?"
    a: "Core Keeper runs seasonal events like the Creepy Costume Party (which unlocks crops such as Grumpkin) and Christmas events with their own workbenches and recipes. Check the Wiki's seasonal pages for details."
  - q: "How do I check my game version?"
    a: "Your exact game version is displayed in the main menu (lower-left corner). Compare it with a mod's tested version before installing, and with the site's 1.3.0.6 baseline when reading guides."
