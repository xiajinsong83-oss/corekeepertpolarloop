faq:
  - q: "How long do crops take to grow in Core Keeper?"
    a: "Every crop takes a fixed 10 minutes from a watered seed to a harvestable plant — 2 minutes to sprout, 4 to grow, and 4 to mature. Watering is required; unwatered seeds don't grow."
  - q: "What is the golden crop chance?"
    a: "There is a base 3% chance for any planted seed to become a golden crop when planted. The Expert Gardener talent adds +3–15% chance to the golden mutation."
  - q: "How does harvest chance work?"
    a: "Each Gardening level adds +0.4% extra harvest chance, up to +40% at level 100. Gear like the Farmer's Hat (+10.1%) and Petal Ring (+22.7%) stack on top, and Kelp Dumpling food adds +15% for 10 minutes."
  - q: "Do seeds come back when I harvest?"
    a: "Characters initially fail to retrieve the seed from about 25% of harvested plants. Putting 5 points into the Grateful gardener talent reduces that failure to keep your seed stock sustainable."
  - q: "What ground types can I plant on?"
    a: "Crops grow on tilled ground matching their requirement: Dirt for most crops, Stone Block for Carrock, Mold Block for Puffungi, Beach Block for coral and beach fruits, and Crystal Block for Sunrice and Lunacorn."
  - q: "Which crop makes the most money?"
    a: "With verified sell values, Bloat Oat, Pinegrapple and Sunrice each sell for 6 gold and give the best profit per hour (36g per plot per hour with self-grown seeds). Heart Berry sells for only 1 gold but is the best for healing potions."
  - q: "Why does my Heart Berry not sell for much?"
    a: "Heart Berry is a utility crop — it's worth far more as a cooking and potion ingredient than as a straight sell. Grow it for the buffs, not the gold."
  - q: "Can I harvest crops automatically?"
    a: "Manual harvesting is required to gain Gardening experience and is the core loop — sprinklers water the crops for you, but you still harvest by hand to level up."
