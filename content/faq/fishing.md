faq:
  - q: "How do I fish in Core Keeper?"
    a: "Craft a fishing rod at the Workbench, equip it, stand next to water and cast. Different water types hold different fish, so move between biomes to complete the compendium."
  - q: "How many water types are there?"
    a: "The fish compendium covers 10 water types — from the Dirt Biome's normal water to the Passage, Sunken Sea, Desert, Crystal and other biome waters. Use the Fishing Filter to see the full list."
  - q: "Which fish are the rarest?"
    a: "Rare fish have the lowest catch chances within their water's pool — entries like the Elder Dragonfish, Tornis Kingfish and Riftian Lampfish are the long grinds. Sort the filter by catch chance to find them."
  - q: "Do fish sell for gold?"
    a: "Some fish sell for a useful amount, but most are worth far more as cooking ingredients — many recipes need specific fish to create food or buff dishes."
  - q: "What is bait for?"
    a: "Lures like the Sweet Lure target specific fish and raise their catch odds. Check the Fishing Guide for lure recipes and which fish they attract."
  - q: "Can I fish anywhere?"
    a: "No — each water type has its own fish table. You need to visit each biome's water to catch its exclusive fish; a fish found in the Dirt Biome won't appear in the Sunken Sea."
