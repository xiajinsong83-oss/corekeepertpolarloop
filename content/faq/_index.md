## Quick answers

- **How long do crops take to grow?** A fixed 10 minutes from watered seed to harvest.
- **What is the boss order?** Glurch → Ghorm → Hive Mother → Malugaz.
- **Where do I install mods?** Steam Workshop (subscribe) or mod.io (download into your mods folder).
- **Do food buffs stack?** Same-type buffs don't stack — only the highest value applies.
