The **Iron Anvil** is the Forgotten Ruins anvil, the workbench for iron- and gold-tier equipment.

## Use

- Crafts the Iron Helm, Iron Breastplate, Iron Pants, Iron Shield, Iron Sword, Iron Halberd and Iron Bow
- Made at the Iron Workbench from 10 Iron Bar
- Also includes all recipes from the Copper and Tin Anvils
