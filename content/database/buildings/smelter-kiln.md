The **Smelter Kiln** is the second-tier ore refiner, used once you have mined past copper, tin and iron.

## Use

- Refines Gold Ore, Scarlet Ore and Octarine Ore into their bars
- Copper, Tin and Iron are still smelted in the basic Furnace; Galaxite needs the Fury Forge
- Runs automatically — add multiple kilns to keep up with automated drill setups
