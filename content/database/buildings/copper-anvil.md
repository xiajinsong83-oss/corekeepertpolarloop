The **Copper Anvil** is the first anvil you build, unlocking copper-tier armor and weapons after the Basic Workbench.

## Use

- Crafts the Copper Helm, Copper Breastplate, Copper Pants, Copper Sword and Wooden Shield
- Made at the Copper Workbench from 8 Copper Bar
- Upgrade path: Tin Anvil → Iron Anvil
