The **Tin Anvil** is the clay-caves anvil, used once you have moved on from copper gear in The Clay Caves.

## Use

- Crafts the Bronze Helm, Bronze Breastplate and Bronze Pants
- Unlocks the Slingshot (first ranged weapon) and Wooden Shield, which blocks 70% of damage when held
- Also includes all recipes from the Copper Anvil
