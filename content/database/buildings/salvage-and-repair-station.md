The **Salvage and Repair Station** is built early and stays useful for the entire playthrough.

## Use

- Repairs the durability of your tools, weapons and armor
- Salvages unwanted gear back into base materials
- Cheap to make (5 Copper Bar) — place it next to your crafting area
