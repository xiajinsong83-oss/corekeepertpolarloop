The **Cooking Pot** is built early and used throughout the whole game to turn raw ingredients into food buffs.

## Use

- Draws ingredients from adjacent chests, so line it up with storage
- Golden ingredients always produce rare (or epic with the Master Chef talent) food
- Cooked food gives damage, mining and healing buffs essential for bosses
