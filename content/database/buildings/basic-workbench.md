The **Basic Workbench** is the first crafting station you build in Core Keeper. It unlocks the initial tool and equipment recipes.

## Use

- Crafts the Copper Pickaxe, Copper Sword and other early items
- Upgrade path: Iron Anvil → Gold Anvil → Scarlet Workbench
