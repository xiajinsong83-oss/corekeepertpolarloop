The **Furnace** is the first ore-processing station in Core Keeper, smelting raw ore into bars for tools and equipment. It's the bridge between mining copper/tin and crafting metal gear.

## What it processes

- Copper Ore → **Copper Bar**
- Tin Ore → **Tin Bar**
- Later: Iron, Gold and Scarlet bars for higher tiers

## The parallel furnace trick

You can't speed up a single furnace's smelting time — but you can build **several furnaces** and process ores concurrently. A row of 3–4 furnaces next to a chest of ore cuts early-game waiting dramatically.

## Tips

- Place furnaces near your workbench so smelting and crafting flow together
- Feed them from a chest; grab bars as they finish
- Upgrade path: Smelting Kilns unlock in later tiers for faster processing
