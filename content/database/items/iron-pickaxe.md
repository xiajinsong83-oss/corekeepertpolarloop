The **Iron Pickaxe** is the tier-3 mining tool in Core Keeper, crafted at the Iron Anvil after you reach The Forgotten Ruins. It unlocks gold and scarlet ore mining, the gateway to late-early-game equipment.

## Use

- Mines **Gold Ore** and **Scarlet Ore**
- Faster on earlier ores than the Tin Pickaxe
- Required before the first three bosses for the recommended Iron gear tier

## Crafting path

1. Copper Pickaxe → mine Tin in The Clay Caves
2. Tin Pickaxe → mine Iron in The Forgotten Ruins
3. Iron Pickaxe → mine Gold / Scarlet for Iron-tier equipment
