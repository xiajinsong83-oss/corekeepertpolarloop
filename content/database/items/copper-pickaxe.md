The **Copper Pickaxe** is the first mining tool in Core Keeper, crafted at the Copper Workbench. It mines copper and tin ore efficiently and is the natural upgrade from the starting tools.

## Use

- Mines **Copper Ore** and **Tin Ore**
- Upgrades to the Tin Pickaxe for iron mining
