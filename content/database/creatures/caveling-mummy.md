**Caveling Mummy** is a hostile enemy mob in Core Keeper. A mold-infected undead caveling wandering The Desert of Beginnings, it is best known for dropping the Zealot's Scimitar, the Puppet Ring and the pajama clothing set.

## Where to find it

It spawns in **The Desert of Beginnings** (mold-infected faction).

## Combat tips

- **High health pool**: 1,836 HP — expect longer fights than ordinary desert mobs
- **Damage value**: not listed on the wiki page (data待核) — treat it as a mid-tier desert threat and don't face-tank unarmored
- **Farm it for gear**: its interesting drops (Zealot's Scimitar, Puppet Ring, pajama set) sit around a ~2.76% drop rate each

## Drops

- **Zealot's Scimitar** (2.76%) — weapon
- **Puppet Ring** (2.76%) — accessory
- **Pajama Shirt / Pajama Cap / Pajama Pants** (2.76% each) — sleepwear cosmetic set
- **Ancient Gemstone** (1.84%)
- **Caveling Mummy Figurine** (0.55%) — placeable collectible
