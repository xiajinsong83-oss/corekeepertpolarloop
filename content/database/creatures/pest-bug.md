**Pest Bug** is a **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Forgotten Ruins**. It is a disgusting insect that moves with uncanny speed, commonly found where there is darkness, dirt and food to offer.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly**, **Dodo** or **Orex** — a handy way to feed farm animals without spending crops.
