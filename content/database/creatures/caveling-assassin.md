The **Caveling Assassin** is a fast Desert caveling that fires a spread of three daggers and then dashes straight through its target. Its high speed and pass-through movement make it hard to pin down.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
