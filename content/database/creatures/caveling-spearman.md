**Caveling Spearman** is a hostile enemy mob in Core Keeper found in The Clay Caves. It is the close-quarters melee counterpart to the spear-throwing Caveling Skirmisher — a low-health early cave mob you will meet constantly while mining clay.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Clay Caves**, respawning on **Clay Moss** tiles.

## Combat tips

- **Treat it as basic fodder**: only 270 HP and a 74-damage melee hit — safe in copper gear once you understand its swing
- **Don't stand in clusters**: Clay Moss spawns it alongside Skirmishers that throw spears from range; kite the group rather than face-tanking
- **Cheap farm target**: reliable early Larva Meat and Fiber source while exploring the caves

## Drops

- **Fiber** (22.91%) — early crafting material
- **Larva Meat** (22.62%) — food/early cooking ingredient
- **Clay Moss** (4.3%) — spawn tile material
