**Earthworm** is a **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Undergrounds**, **The Clay Caves**, and **Azeos' Wilderness**. A worm — nothing special about it.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly** or **Dodo**. It is also used as a crafting material at the **Fishing Workbench** (with a Heart Berry to craft Sweet Lure).
