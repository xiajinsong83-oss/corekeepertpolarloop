The **Colossal Amoeba** is a huge hostile blob that drifts through **The Passage**. With 5,535 HP it is one of the tankiest mobs of the Passage region.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Passage**.

## Combat tips

- **Extremely high health (5,535 HP)** — commit before engaging; bring crowd control
- Wiki page is currently a stub: **attack damage not yet documented** (数据待核)
- Reliable source of **Cytoplasm** and the crafting item **Sulfur Bomb**

## Drops

- **Cytoplasm** — 85.11% (3–5)
- **Sulfur Bomb** — 12.77%
- **Squishy Egg** — 1.28%; **Colossal Amoeba Figurine** — 0.85%
