The **Caveling Brute** is a slow but hard-hitting melee enemy found in The Undergrounds and The Clay Caves. It hits much harder than a normal Caveling — don't tank its swings.

## Combat tips

- **Dodge, don't block**: its swings are telegraphed; sidestep rather than trading hits
- **Kite it**: it's slower than the player, so attack between its swing cooldowns
- **Iron gear comfort**: with 140 HP and 16 damage, it's manageable in copper but much safer in tin+

## Drops

- **Caveling Remains** (common) — salvage/repair material
- **Iron Ore** (common) — early source before you reach The Forgotten Ruins
