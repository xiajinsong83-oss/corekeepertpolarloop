The **Electro-Pest** is a rare hostile pest found in **The Forgotten Ruins**. It belongs to the **Abiotic Factor Facility** scene — don't confuse it with the friendly pet **Electro-Pet**.

## Where to find it

It spawns inside the **Abiotic Factor Facility** scene in The Forgotten Ruins. **Only 8 Electro-Pests exist per world and they never respawn**, so treat each encounter as limited.

## Combat tips

- **Limited per world** — do not waste the encounter; it will not come back
- Wiki page is currently a stub: **attack damage not yet documented** (数据待核)
- It drops event-themed gear such as the **Quill Rifle** and **Bottled Anomaly**

## Drops

- **Scrap Parts** — 57.54%
- **Fiber** — 23.01%; **Pest Egg** — 11.51%
- **Bottled Anomaly** / **Quill Rifle** / **Makeshift Goggles** — 2.3% each
- **Electro-Pest Figurine** — 1.04%
