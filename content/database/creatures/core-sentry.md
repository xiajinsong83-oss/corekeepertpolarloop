The **Core Sentry** is a large Forlorn Metropolis construct that stays dormant until you approach, then powers up and stomps a three-way fork of bright projectiles. Very high health makes it a priority target.

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
