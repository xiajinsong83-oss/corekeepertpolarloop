**Dream Messenger** is a flying **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed. It is said to be a bringer of good dreams and is always seen among sleeping creatures.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **Azeos' Wilderness**.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly** or **Dodo**.
