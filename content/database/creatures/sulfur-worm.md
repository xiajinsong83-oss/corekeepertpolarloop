The **Sulfur Worm** is a hostile worm that burrows through **The Passage**. It is one of the signature late-area mobs of the Passage, alongside the Colossal Amoeba.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Passage**.

## Combat tips

- **Tanky (3,075 HP)** — expect a longer fight than earlier biomes
- Wiki page is currently a stub: **attack damage not yet documented** (数据待核)
- Its drops are the basic Passage crafting materials **Cytoplasm** and **Sulfur Bud**

## Drops

- **Cytoplasm** — 49.63% (1–2); **Sulfur Bud** — 49.63% (1–2)
- **Sulfur Worm Figurine** — 0.74%
