The **Caveling Skirmisher** is a ranged mob of The Clay Caves that hurls spears on sight from Clay Moss tiles. Weaker than melee cavelings but annoying at range, it drops Larva Meat and Fiber when farmed.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
