The **Caveling Gardener** patrols Azeos' Wilderness on Lush Moss, circling players before striking. While standing on grass it can sprout up to five Snare Plants that trap you in place.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
