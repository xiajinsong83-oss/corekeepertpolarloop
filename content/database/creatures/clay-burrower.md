The **Clay Burrower** is an aggressive burrowing enemy found in **The Clay Caves**. It is a larva-faction mob with a rounded, earthen body.

## Where to find it

It spawns in **The Clay Caves**.

## Combat tips

- It is an **aggressive (hostile)** mob with 405 HP
- **Attack numbers are not listed on the wiki page** — treat its melee hits as mid-range Clay Caves damage and dodge normally
- The wiki rolls **1–2 drops per kill**, so loot per fight can vary

## Drops

- **Marbled Meat** — 28.99%; **Root Seed** — 28.99%
- **Slingshot** — 5.8%
- **Clay Burrower Figurine** — 2.9%
- **Pot Lid** — 2.17%
