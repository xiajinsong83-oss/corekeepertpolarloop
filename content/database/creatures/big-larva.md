The **Big Larva** is an aggressive hostile worm of **The Clay Caves** and the **Larva Hive**. It is a larger cousin of the common Larva and will chew through your base if left unchecked.

## Where to find it

It spawns in **The Clay Caves** and the **Larva Hive Biome**, respawning on **Chrysalis** tiles.

## Combat tips

- **Larva aggro on torches first** — they prefer lit player objects over other targets, and will attack many player-placed base buildings
- **Melee deals 93 damage** with 412 HP; keep your distance and kite
- **Immune to slow from slime** and **immune to acid damage**
- Because it targets torches, it can be lured away from your camp

## Drops

- **Larva Meat** — 82.79%
- **Grub Kapok Seed** — 2.52%
- **Shiny Larva Meat** — 0.5%
