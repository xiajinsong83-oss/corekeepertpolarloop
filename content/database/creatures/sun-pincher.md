**Sun Pincher** is a **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Sunken Sea**. This red-yellow crustacean always has its claws raised, which gives it a seemingly happy expression.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly**, **Dodo** or **Orex** — a handy way to feed farm animals without spending crops.
