**Sniffling** is a peaceful **critter** in Core Keeper, added with **The Void & Voltage** update. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed. It is a skittish little rodent that scurries around for hardened crumbs left in debris.

## Where to find it

Data pending verification（数据待核）— the atma.gg page confirms it was added with The Void & Voltage update but does not yet list a specific spawn biome.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly** or **Dodo**.
