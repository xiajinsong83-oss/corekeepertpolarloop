The **Shrooman** is a weak mushroom enemy found in **The Undergrounds**. It has little health and slowly charges at anyone who wanders onto its fungal ground.

## Where to find it

It respawns periodically on **Fungal Soil** in The Undergrounds. Larger **Shrooman Brutes** patrol the same ground.

## Combat tips

- **Easy pickings** — only 105 HP and a 39-damage dash ram
- Let it charge, sidestep, and hit after it whiffs
- Farm it for early **Mushroom** and seed drops

## Drops

- **Mushroom** (45.99%) — food/crafting
- **Scrap Parts** (10.7%) and **Fungal Soil** (5.35%)
- **Heart Berry / Bomb Pepper / Glow Tulip seeds** (10.7% each)
- Rare: **Shrooman Cap** (1.07%), **Witch Doctor** gear, **Shrooman Figurine** (0.32%)
