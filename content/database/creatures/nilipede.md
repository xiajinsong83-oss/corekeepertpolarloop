The **Nilipede** is a hostile crystal creature that prowls **The Shimmering Frontier**. A long, gem-encrusted centipede with a lot of HP, it is one of the late-frontier mobs you will run into alongside Mimites.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Shimmering Frontier**.

## Combat tips

- **High health pool (2,670 HP)** — bring your best frontier-tier weapon
- Wiki page is currently a stub: **attack damage not yet documented** (数据待核)
- Farming it is the source of the **Ninja** armor set and the **Ricochet Shuriken**

## Drops

- **Marbled Meat** — 26.32%; **Gleam Wood Seed** — 26.32%
- **Ricochet Shuriken** — 5.26%
- **Ninja Cowl / Ninja Garments / Ninja Leggings** — 3.95% each
- **Nilipede Figurine** — 2.63%
