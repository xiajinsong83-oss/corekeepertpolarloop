The **Caveling** is the basic hostile mob of The Undergrounds, found near the starting area. Low health and telegraphed attacks make it an easy first combat target.

## Tips

- Attacks are telegraphed — dodge sideways, not backwards
- Drops Caveling Remains used in early crafting
