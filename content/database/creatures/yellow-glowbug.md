The **Yellow Glowbug** is a flying **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net**. This one drifts in swarms that look like dancing lights and glows yellow.

## Where to find it

It appears in swarms in **The Undergrounds**, **The Clay Caves**, and **The Desert of Beginnings**. Its yellow glow is specific to these biomes.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- It is **stationary** once placed, and **emits yellow light** — a free, slot-free light source for your base
- Caught glowbugs can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration, but they still move around and die in a single hit if attacked
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Glowbugs are the one critter **cattle will not eat** — **Strolly Poly**, **Dodo**, and **Orex** eat all critters except glowbugs, so keep them as lighting rather than feed.
