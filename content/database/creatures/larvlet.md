**Larvlet** is a **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Clay Caves**. Destroying a **Cocoon** also has a 50% chance to spawn between 2 and 5 Larvlets. It is a tiny larva that doesn't harm you but likes to be collected and held in a warm hand.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly** or **Dodo**. It is also used as a crafting material at the **Fishing Workbench** (with a Mushroom to craft Sticky Lure).
