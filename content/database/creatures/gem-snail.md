**Gem Snail** is a **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed.

## Where to find it

It slowly crawls through the dangers of **The Shimmering Frontier** — a small snail carrying a gleaming gem-like shell.

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1
- **Loot when caught**: Crystal Snail (~10% per roll, 1–2 rolls); Large Sun Crystal (~7.58% per roll, 2–3 rolls)

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly** or **Dodo** — a handy way to feed farm animals without spending crops.
