The **Red Slime** is the always-aggressive early slime of The Undergrounds and The Clay Caves. Unlike the passive Orange Slime it shares Ground Slime with, it will hop onto you on sight and is immune to slow and acid.

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
