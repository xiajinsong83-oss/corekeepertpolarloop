The **Bomb Scarab** is a flying hostile scarab of **The Desert of Beginnings**. It dives through players, then — once hurt badly enough — channels and detonates, breaking nearby walls and objects.

## Where to find it

It spawns in **The Desert of Beginnings**, rarely respawning on **Desert Sand Ground** inside the desert biome. Scarab ambushes also burrow out of the ground in groups, and **Ra-Akar the Sand Titan** summons them as adds during the boss fight.

## Combat tips

- It can **fly over void and water tiles** — bridges and pit traps do not stop it
- **Melee dash deals 236 damage**; once its HP gets low it **hunkers down and explodes** after a short channel — **back off** the moment it squats
- The explosion **breaks walls and objects** in a small area
- Immune to slime slow and acid damage

## Drops

- **Galaxite Ore** — 22.26%; **Scrap Parts** — 22.26%
- **Blasting Dung** — 10.69% (1–3); **Bomb** — 8.9% (3–5)
- **Plume Ball** — 4.45%; **Scarab Wingcover** — 2.23%
