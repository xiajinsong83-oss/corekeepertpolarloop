The **Mold Tentacle** is a hostile mold mob found in the **Mold Biome** of **Azeos' Wilderness**. It glows faintly in the dark, so you can spot it long before it spots you — and it lob projectiles that leave players stunned.

## Where to find it

It spawns in the **Mold Biome** (a sub-biome of Azeos' Wilderness), respawning on **Mold Ground**.

## Combat tips

- Its ranged attack deals **173 damage** and **hard-stuns** for 1–2 seconds on hit
- **Several tentacles together can stun-lock you**, buying time for **Infected Cavelings** to close to melee range — kill tentacles first
- Immune to **mold infection**
- Faint glow gives away its position in the dark; engage from range

## Drops

- **Ancient Coin** — 13.51%
- **Fungal Bone** — 6.76%; **Puffungi Seed** — 6.76%
- **Tome of the Dead** — 1.35%; **Blowpipe** — 1.35%
- **Mold Ring** / **Mold Vein Necklace** / **Mold Tentacle Figurine** — 0.68% each
- **67.57% chance nothing**
