**Caveling Hunter** is a hostile enemy mob in Core Keeper. These sneaky snipers temporarily disguise themselves as a Wilderness Bush, then snipe players with a Flintlock Musket from surprisingly long range — sometimes almost off-screen.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **Azeos' Wilderness**, respawning periodically on **Lush Moss** tiles.

## Combat tips

- **Watch the bushes**: it pretends to be a bush until you get close — scan suspicious foliage before walking past
- **Close the distance**: it wants to keep range; the musket projectile is fast but the rate of fire is slow, so dash between shots
- **Ranged answers work too**: return fire from beyond its preferred musket band while it keeps kiting
- **870 HP, 152 range damage**: don't let it chip you from off-screen while you fight other mobs

## Drops

- **Lush Moss** (2.97%) — spawn tile material
- **Ancient Feather** (2.25%) — wilderness crafting material
- **Ancient Gemstone** (1.78%) — early gem currency
