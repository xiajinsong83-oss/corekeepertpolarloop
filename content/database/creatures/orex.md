**Orex** is a **cattle** (farm animal) in Core Keeper. Unlike small critters, it is not caught with a Bug Net — it is raised as livestock and fed.

## Where to find it

Found in the **Moss Sub Biome** in **The Forgotten Ruins**. This cavernous carnivore has a surprisingly timid temper for having such sharp horns; its large serrated muzzle is sooner to swallow critters whole than to bite the hand that may feed it.

## How to catch

Orex is **cattle**, a farm animal — it cannot be caught with a **Bug Net** like small critters. It is encountered in the wild Moss Sub Biome and kept as livestock.
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

As livestock, **Orex will eat all critters**, except **glowbugs**. In return it drops **Marbled Meat** (100%) and produces **Orex Truffle**.
