**Blackbug** is a **critter** in Core Keeper. Unlike hostile mobs, critters are peaceful creatures you can catch with a **Bug Net** and keep or feed.

## Where to find it

It frequently spawns when destroying wooden objects — breaking **Wood**, **Coral Wood** or **Gleam Wood** has a 15% chance to spawn one, while breaking wooden crates (such as Wooden Crates and Large Crates) has a 30% chance to spawn 1–2 (or 5–8 from large crates).

## How to catch

- Equip a **Bug Net** and swing at it as you walk close
- Critters can be **placed back in the world** as persistent decoration
- **Sell value**: 1

## Feeding

Can be fed to **Strolly Poly**, **Dodo** or **Orex**. It is also used as a crafting material at the **Fishing Workbench** (with a Bomb Pepper to craft Spicy Lure).
