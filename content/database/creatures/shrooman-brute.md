The **Shrooman Brute** is a large, aggressive mushroom enemy of **The Undergrounds**. It lumbers toward you, then charges — and its hard shell makes it surprisingly tanky.

## Where to find it

It spawns in **The Undergrounds**, respawning periodically on **Fungal Soil**.

## Combat tips

- It **charges slowly** at opponents, dealing damage and knockback on impact
- Its **shell reduces incoming damage to 1** while intact — be patient and chip it down
- **If it rams a wall, chasm, or object while charging, it destroys those blocks and becomes stunned and vulnerable** — bait charges into terrain
- It **will not charge across a chasm** — gaps are a safe barrier
- **Charge attack damage is not listed on the wiki page** (marked 数据待核)

## Drops

- **Mushroom** — 53.05% (3–5); **Stoneskin Potion** — 26.53%
- **Giant Mushroom** — 2.65%
