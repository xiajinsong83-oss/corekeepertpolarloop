**Blue Slime** is a hostile enemy mob in Core Keeper found in The Sunken Sea. It hops at you like other slimes, but its hits apply a slippery movement debuff that knocks both players and opposing mobs skidding across the ground.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in **The Sunken Sea**, respawning periodically on **Ground Slippery Slime** tiles.

## Combat tips

- **Stay off the slippery tiles when fighting**: with 1,350 HP and 194 jump damage, it is far tougher than early slimes — don't get knocked around on its home turf
- **Slippery on hit**: it knocks you back and applies slippery movement, which can slide you into other Sunken Sea hazards
- **Immunities**: immune to slime slow and slippery movement itself

## Drops

- **Slippery Slime** (94.79%) — Sunken Sea slime crafting material
