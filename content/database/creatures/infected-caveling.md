**Infected Caveling** is a hostile enemy mob in Core Keeper. A mold-corrupted caveling that walks very slowly like a zombie, then lunges a short distance to melee. Its hits can poison you — a debuff that greatly reduces healing received.

## Where to find it

It spawns naturally in the **Mold Biome** sub-areas of **Azeos' Wilderness**, respawning periodically on **Mold Ground** tiles.

## Combat tips

- **Slow but damaging**: it lumbers toward you, then lunges — 194 melee damage is no joke; keep your distance and kite
- **Mind the poison**: hits have a chance to apply poison, cutting healing received by 75% — don't rely on potions to save you mid-fight
- **It's immune to mold infection**, so mold-based tricks won't work on it

## Drops

- **Scrap Parts** (8.61%) — salvage material
- **Puffungi Seed** (2.15%) — mold biome seed
- **Fungal Bone** (2.15%) — mold biome crafting material
