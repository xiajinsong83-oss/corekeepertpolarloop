The **Purple Slime** is the poison slime of Azeos' Wilderness, spawning on Ground Poison Slime. Its jumps apply poison that cuts healing received by 75%, so keep antidotes or a Remedaisy Necklace ready.

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
