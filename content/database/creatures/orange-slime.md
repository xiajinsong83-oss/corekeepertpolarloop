**Orange Slime** (also called a slime blob, or just "slime") is a neutral enemy mob in Core Keeper. It is passive until attacked — one of the least dangerous enemies in the game thanks to its low damage and passivity.

## Where to find it

It spawns on **Ground Slime** tiles in **The Undergrounds** and **The Clay Caves**.

## Combat tips

- **Leave it alone**: it won't attack until hit, so you can walk past safely
- **When provoked**: it hops on you for 31 jump damage — trivial, but it will also aggro onto your Spike Traps, making them free slime cleaners
- **Immunities**: immune to slime slow and acid damage

## Drops

- **Slime** (58.52%) — basic crafting material, early slime farming target
