The **Tentacle** is a hostile sea creature found only in **The Sunken Sea**. It lobs small white jellyfish projectiles at range, hitting for 194 damage.

## Where to find it

It spawns on **water tiles** in the Sea biome, most often on water adjacent to ground tiles (not bridges). It is rare in open water.

## Combat tips

- **Close the distance** — its jellyfish projectile is painful at range, so swim/bridge toward it
- With 945 HP it takes a few hits; use mid-tier sea gear (Octarine+) for comfort
- Fight from shore or on bridges to avoid being surrounded by other sea mobs

## Drops

- **Octarine Ore** (13.83%) and **Coral Wood** (13.83%) — Sunken Sea materials
- **Tentacle Whip** (1.38%) — whip weapon
- **Sea Shell** (1.38%)
- **Tentacle Figurine** (0.41%) — collectible trophy
