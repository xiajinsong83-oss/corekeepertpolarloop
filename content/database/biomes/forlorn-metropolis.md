The **Forlorn Metropolis** is a ruined hi-tech dungeon scattered through The Sunken Sea. Caveling Scholars support the heavier Core Sentries here, and the ruins hold Octarine Ore and materials for the Phantom Spark.

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
