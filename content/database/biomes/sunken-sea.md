**The Sunken Sea** is the water-filled outer biome of Beach Sand islands and glowing crystal water, mined for Octarine Ore. Tentacles, Blue Slimes and Bubble Crabs apply a slippery debuff, Omoroth lurks ~1100 tiles out, and Forlorn Metropolis dungeons sit among the islands.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
