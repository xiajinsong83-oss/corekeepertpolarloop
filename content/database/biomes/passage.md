**The Passage** is the outermost biome beyond the three outer rings, its fossil-block walls needing late-game mining gear. It yields Pandorium Ore and is home to Sulfur Worms and Urschleim, the optional late-game slime boss circling ~1250 tiles out.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
