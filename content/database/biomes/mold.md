The **Mold Biome** is a fungus-overrun dungeon embedded in Azeos' Wilderness. Mold Ground slows you by up to 75% at ten stacks, and the zombie-like Infected Cavelings plus stunning Mold Tentacles make it one of the hardest areas in the game.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
