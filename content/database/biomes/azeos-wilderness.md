**Azeos' Wilderness** is the lush forest outer-ring biome, mined for Scarlet Ore. Caveling Gardeners and Bush-disguised Caveling Hunters roam with Purple Slimes, Azeos the Sky Titan awaits ~600 tiles out, and Mold dungeons infect parts of it.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
