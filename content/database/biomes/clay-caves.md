**The Clay Caves** is one of the two inner-ring biomes (150–450 tiles from the Core), home to the game's **tin ore** and the **Larva Hive** dungeon where the Hive Mother waits.

## Location

Cross the chasm around The Undergrounds with a Wood Bridge. The Clay Caves and The Forgotten Ruins each occupy one half of the world beyond the chasm — the exact angle varies by **seed**, so if you find the Ruins first, walk the opposite direction from the Core.

## What you find

- **Tin Ore** — the tier-2 metal, for tin tools and equipment
- **Clay** — building material
- **Iron Ore** — occasional veins
- **Larva Hive entrance** — sub-biome dungeon with the Hive Mother at ~330 tiles

## Tips

- Bring a Tin Pickaxe recipe — you'll craft it here
- Clear the Hive with Iron gear recommended; use the Hive Mother Scanner to locate it
- Use the [Seed Finder](/tools/seed-finder/) to preview which direction the Clay Caves sit in your seed
