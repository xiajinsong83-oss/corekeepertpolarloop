**Breaker's Reach** is the mechanical, void-tainted late-game biome found far north past The Passage, added in the Void & Voltage update. It yields Relucite Ore and Corrupted Alloy and is overrun by Geobots and void cavelings.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
