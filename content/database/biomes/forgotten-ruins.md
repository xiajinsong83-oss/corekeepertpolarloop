**The Forgotten Ruins** is the stone inner-ring biome opposite The Clay Caves, mined for Iron Ore and Gold Ore. Cavelings defend their settlements here on Stone Moss, and Malugaz the Corrupted is summoned in the throne room around 350 tiles from The Core.

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
