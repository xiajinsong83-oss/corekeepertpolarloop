The **Molten Quarry** is a volcanic sub-biome of The Desert of Beginnings, its blackened terrain split by lava and burning Ground Magma Slime. It is home to Igneous the Molten Mass and the Ancient Forge needed for the Soul Seeker Pickaxe.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
