The **Shimmering Frontier** is a bright blue crystal ring wrapped around the outer biomes. It is the source of Solarite Ore, Gleam Wood and the colored gems, patrolled by Crystal Snails, Mimites and Orbital Turrets.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
