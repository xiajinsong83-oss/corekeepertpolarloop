**The Undergrounds** (the Dirt Biome) is the safe starting ring around The Core, spanning 0–140 tiles. It is also the default biome filling the gaps between other biomes.

## What you find

- Copper and tin ore for the first tools
- Wood, stone and early food crops
- The first three bosses' summon points at fixed arcs

## Tips

- Build your first base within 140 tiles of the Core for safety
- Follow Ghorm's track to explore outward efficiently
