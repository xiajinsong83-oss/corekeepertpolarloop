The **Larva Hive Biome** is a maze-like dungeon inside The Clay Caves, its walls made of tough organic Larva Hive Block. Slimes do not spawn here; instead Chrysalis and acid-laying larvae are everywhere, and The Hive Mother waits at its heart.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
