**The Desert of Beginnings** is the hot sandy outer-ring biome, mined for Galaxite Ore. Explosive Bomb Scarabs and swift Caveling Assassins patrol, Ra-Akar the Sand Titan waits ~1000 tiles out, and Molten Quarry and Oasis sub-biomes are hidden among the sandstone.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
