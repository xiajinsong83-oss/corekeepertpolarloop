**The Great Wall** is an unbreakable wavy barrier spanning 450–520 tiles around the inner biomes. It gates access to the outer biomes until you have powered up The Core and unlocked the ability to break it down.

## Tips

- Data sourced from the Core Keeper wiki (atma.gg); numbers shift with patches
- Bring appropriate resistance gear before farming this area
