**Malugaz the Corrupted** is an optional boss in the Forgotten Ruins, summoned at the Corrupted Core. It drops Malugaz's Skull and a Corrupted Chest.

## Strategy

- Bring ranged options for its telegraphed attacks
- Optional but drops strong corrupted gear
