**Ghorm the Devourer** is the second main boss, a giant worm that circles the world ring. Follow its track in the dirt ring and summon it with the Ghorm Summoning Idol.

## Strategy

- **Ride the ring**: chase it down in a straight line rather than waiting
- Its horn is required for progression
