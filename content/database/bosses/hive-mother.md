The **Hive Mother** is the third main boss, deep inside the Larva Hive dungeon in the Clay Caves. Clear the hive to its arena at roughly 330 tiles.

## Strategy

- **Iron gear recommended** — its larva swarm hits from all sides
- Use the Hive Mother Scanner to locate the arena
