**Glurch the Abominable Mass** is the first main boss, summoned at The Core or found in the first ring. Beating it unlocks the Glurch Eye required for progression and drops early metals.

## Strategy

- **Early gear is fine** — dodge its slam, attack after the shockwave
- Bring a Copper-tier weapon and food buffs
