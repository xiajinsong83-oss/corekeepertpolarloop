Food in Core Keeper isn't just hunger — the right dishes are pre-boss loadouts. This cooking guide explains how food buffs work, which ingredients give the strongest effects, and the best combos before each fight.

## How food buffs work

- **Food value** refills your hunger; hunger loss only reduces max health and stats — you can't die of starvation.
- Cooked ingredients give **Regen, max health, armor, damage and speed buffs** with a duration.
- Buffs from **different food slots stack**; stacking the same buff type applies the highest value (buff stacking in Core Keeper takes the strongest effect, not a sum).
- Ingredient quality (Regular / Rare / Epic) scales every number.

## Ingredient buff reference

| Ingredient | Food | Key buffs (Regular → Epic) |
|---|---|---|
| Mushroom | +19→+28 | +4.2→+6.3 HP/s for 20s |
| Heart Berry | +19→+28 | +25→+38 max HP (10 min), +2.8→+4.2 HP/s (20s) |
| Golden Heart Berry | +18→+27 | +50→+75 max HP, +4.4→+6.6 HP/s, +17.1→+25.6% melee damage (10 min) |
| Bloat Oat | +43→+64 | +9.1→+13.6 HP/s for 20s (biggest single food value) |
| Carrock | +15→+22 | +23→+34 armor (10 min) |
| Golden Carrock | +13→+20 | +26→+39 armor + knockback chance |
| Bomb Pepper | +10→+15 | +21→+31.5% movement speed (1 min) |
| Golden Bomb Pepper | +11→+16 | +26.9→+40.4% move speed + melee attack speed (10 min) |
| Puffungi | +10→+15 | −6→−9% boss damage taken + HP/s |
| Golden Puffungi | +10→+15 | −9→−14% boss damage taken + up to +21% boss damage (1 min) |
| Glow Tulip | +2→+3 | +1.4→+2.1 mana/s + glow (10 min) |
| Riftian Lampfish | +19→+28 | +7.2→+10.8 HP/s, +27.6→+41.4% magic damage, +58→+87 max mana |

Cooking Pot recipes combine ingredients; the Chef background starts with a Cooking Pot and 8 Mushroom, plus +3 Cooking skill.

## Best food before bosses

| Fight | Loadout |
|---|---|
| Glurch (first boss) | Heart Berry (max HP + regen) + Carrock (armor) |
| Ghorm (track boss) | Golden Bomb Pepper (move speed + attack speed) + Puffungi |
| Hive Mother (dungeon) | Golden Puffungi (boss damage + mitigation) + Heart Berry |
| Azeos / Omoroth | Golden Heart Berry + Golden Carrock; magic builds add Riftian Lampfish |

Keep cooked food in a chest by the arena and re-apply before the fight — regen and short buffs run out mid-encounter.

## FAQ

**Do food buffs stack in Core Keeper?** Different buff slots stack; the same buff type takes the highest value rather than summing.

**What's the best healing food?** Bloat Oat gives the biggest food value and strongest HP/s; Heart Berry adds max health on top.

**How do I get better food ingredients?** Gardening grows crops (including Heart Berries), fishing catches fish (Riftian Lampfish in the Sunken Sea), and mushrooms spawn around the underground.
