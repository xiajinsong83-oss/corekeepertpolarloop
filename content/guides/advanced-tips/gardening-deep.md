A greenhouse is where Core Keeper shifts from "scavenge food" to "manufacture buffs." This advanced guide covers the growth cycle, picking crops by ground type, stacking harvest chance for throughput, and laying out plots so watering never becomes a chore.

## The 10-minute growth cycle

Once you water a seed, the timer runs on its own — you can leave and come back:

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Watered seed → sprout | 2 minutes |
| Sprout → pre-harvest | 4 minutes |
| Pre-harvest → harvestable | 4 minutes |

Every manual harvest grants gardening experience. You start failing to recover the seed on about 1 in 4 harvests until you put points into the **Grateful Gardener** talent, which returns seeds more reliably.

## Pick crops by the ground you can build

Most crops grow on any tilled ground, but the valuable endgame crops demand special blocks. Plan your greenhouse zones around what you can lay down:

| Crop | Harvest | Required ground | Why grow it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart Berry | Heart Berry | Any | Early healing cooking |
| Bomb Pepper | Bomb Pepper | Any | Move-speed buff food — boss food |
| Glow Tulip | Glow Tulip | Any | Light + lure ingredient |
| Bloat Oat | Bloat Oat | Any | Staple grain |
| Grub Kapok | Fiber | Any | Passive fiber income |
| Root Seed | Wood | Any | Self-spreading wood |
| Carrock | Carrock | Stone Block | Armor-boost cooking |
| Puffungi | Puffungi | Mold Block | Cooking ingredient |
| Coral / Pewpaya / Pinegrapple | Wood / fruit | Beach Block | Sunken Sea-era crops |
| Sunrice / Lunacorn | Sunrice / Lunacorn | Crystal Block | Shimmering Frontier-era crops |
| Grumpkin | Grumpkin | Any | Creepy Costume Party event only |

## Stacking harvest chance (the profit lever)

Harvest chance is what turns a 10-minute cycle into real throughput — it rolls for double, and then triple, crops on every harvest. Stack every source:

| Source | Harvest chance |
|---|---|
| Gardening skill | +0.4% per level (40% at level 100) |
| Farmer's Hat | 10.1% (20.8% at level 19) |
| Petal Ring | 22.7% each — **two rings stack to 45.4%** |
| Eostre Necklace | 10.3% (19.1% at level 19) |
| Kelp Dumpling (food) | +15% for 10 minutes |

At the fully stacked maximum (leveled, reinforced gear + the dumpling) you reach **182.4% harvest chance**: two crops per harvest guaranteed, plus an 82.4% chance at a third. Without a single gardening level, just wearing the gear already gives 67.3%.

## Golden crops and greenhouse layout

Golden crops roll at a **base 3% per plant**, boosted by the Expert Gardener talent (+3–15%). They are the premium cooking ingredients, so the profit move is to grow the *cooking staples* you use every run — Bomb Pepper, Heart Berry, Carrock — at scale, and let golden versions of those be your real payout.

Layout tips:

- **Zone by ground type.** Keep the "any ground" staples together, and build separate Stone / Mold / Beach / Crystal bays for the special crops so you are not mixing blocks.
- **Automate watering.** Sprinklers remove the manual Watering Can pass — otherwise a large plot becomes a second job.
- **Batch by cycle.** Because every crop shares the same 10-minute timer, plant whole zones at once so you harvest, re-till and re-water in a single sweep.

## FAQ

**How long does a crop take to grow?** A watered seed takes 10 minutes total: 2 minutes to sprout, 4 minutes to pre-harvest, then 4 minutes to harvestable.

**What increases golden crop chance?** The base golden chance is 3%. The Expert Gardener talent adds +3–15%, and leveling raises your odds further. Golden crops are the high-value cooking ingredients.

**How do I get double harvests?** Stack harvest chance from Gardening level (+0.4% per level), Farmer's Hat, two Petal Rings, Eostre Necklace and Kelp Dumpling. At the 182.4% maximum you guarantee two crops per harvest with an 82.4% shot at a third.
