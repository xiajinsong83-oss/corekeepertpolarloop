The [boss guide](/guides/combat-bosses/boss-guide/) covers where each boss lives, how to summon it and what it drops. This strategy guide is the fight itself: the health pools, the attack numbers, the enrage thresholds, and the positioning that turns a wipe into a farm.

## Rules that apply to almost every boss

- **Enrage at a threshold.** Most bosses switch to a faster, deadlier phase once you push them below a health percentage — the fight gets *harder* near the end, not easier.
- **Reset distance.** Walk too far away and the boss returns to spawn and heals to full. Know the leash before you kite.
- **Status immunities.** Nearly every boss is immune to stun and slippery movement, and the early bosses are immune to slime slow. Don't waste builds on them.
- **Movement speed is your best armor.** Cooked Bomb Pepper food (+move speed) out-dodges almost every attack in the game.

## Early Core bosses

### Glurch the Abominable Mass
Glurch flashes white right before each jump — that is your dodge cue. Stay in front and keep hitting, kite away on the flash, then re-engage on landing. It enrages at 30% and jumps faster. **Cheese option:** stand exactly 14 tiles from its hit-box (13 clear tiles between you) and it will neither aggro nor reset, letting a ranged weapon and 5 Spike Traps on the rune do the work.

### Ghorm the Devourer
Ghorm never stops circling the Core (one orbit every 4 minutes) and cannot be slowed. Cover its track with **100–120 Spike Traps** to burst it to 70%, then enter the "bullfight" phase — bait its charge through the remaining traps while you chip in. For a cleaner fight, mine a bridge or lay a rail along the inside of the track and attack while running alongside. The piercing **Grubzooka** (from Hive Mother) is the fastest farming weapon once unlocked.

### The Hive Mother
She only aggros after you hit her, so prep first. Her acid mortar (120) leaves damaging Ground Acid Slime — cook **Green Blister Head** food for 5 minutes of acid immunity, or lay Rug / Woven Mat / Rail over the acid to walk on it. Safest setup: dig a trench around her chamber and snipe from 13 tiles beyond her hit-box, where her mortar can't reach. Enrages at 60% and spawns cocoon adds faster.

## Outer-ring bosses

### Malugaz the Corrupted (optional)
Two forms, and it **restores to full health** when it transforms — you must burn through both. Form one teleports and rains fireballs (152) and ground fires that last 30 seconds; form two chases and charges. Bring **at least 30 Healing Potions** and Stoneskin Potions, Scarlet gear, and a shield for the charge. **Cheese:** flood the arena with water and fight from a Boat/Speeder — flames can't spawn on the water.

### Azeos the Sky Titan
All thunder beams (130), no melee — but it teleports around and its random beam pattern is the hardest to dodge. Fill in the arena gaps to enlarge the floor. When Azeos summons **healing crystals**, swap to your pickaxe and smash them immediately, or it heals itself on contact. Enrages at 40% and fires beams faster.

### Omoroth the Sea Titan
Summoned by fishing an Expert Lure off a whirlpool. Fight **ranged from a Speeder boat** — tornadoes (215) pull you in and tentacles (258) guard your feet. Before 50% enrage, kill every tentacle it spawns; after enrage, stop wasting time on them and **hug the arena edge** so tentacles spawn at (or outside) the rim, then burn Omoroth down.

### Ra-Akar the Sand Titan
The largest health pool in the game (160,799). Homing orbs (258) track you — **run and dash, don't try to block them**. Sand explosions come in inward, outward and clockwise rings; read the ring and slip through the gap. Enrages at 50% and attacks faster. Reinforced Galaxite gear and high sustain are mandatory.

## FAQ

**What is the hardest boss in Core Keeper?** By health, Ra-Akar the Sand Titan (160,799 HP) is the largest, but Malugaz is often called the hardest early option because it has two forms and resets instantly if you leave its small arena.

**Why do my traps not work on Ghorm?** Ghorm cannot be slowed or trapped — it lays fresh dirt and slime under itself as it moves. Traps are a *burst* tool: cover its track to drop it to 70% health, then bait its charge through the remaining traps.

**Should I melee or ranged the titans?** Azeos, Omoroth and Ra-Akar are best fought ranged or on a mount — their area attacks (beams, tornadoes, homing orbs) punish standing still. Melee works for Glurch, Ghorm's tail and Malugaz's charge windows.
