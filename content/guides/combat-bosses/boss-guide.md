Core Keeper's boss order follows your tool progression: three bosses around the Core must fall before it powers up, then the outer-ring titans open endgame content. This Core Keeper boss guide lists every boss, where they spawn, how to summon them and what they drop.

## The three early bosses (power up the Core)

Defeat all three and place their drops on the effigies next to the Core to activate it.

| Boss | Location | Drops | Recommended gear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glurch the Abominable Mass | ~65 tiles from Core, random angle; screen shakes nearby | Glurch Eye (100%), Copper/Tin Ore, Copper Ring | Tin or better |
| Ghorm the Devourer | ~250 tiles; circles the Core on an 8-tile-wide track | Ghorm's Horn (100%), Iron Ore, Ancient Gem, Ghorm's Jaw | Iron |
| Hive Mother | ~330 tiles in Larva Hive (Clay Caves sub-biome) | Heart of the Hive Mother (100%), Larva Hive Chest, Golden Larva pet (rare) | Iron |

**Boss 1 — Glurch**: a slow melee slime. Keep distance, dodge its jumps, and don't let it corner you against walls. Tin gear is comfortable; the fight is a movement check more than a DPS check.

**Boss 2 — Ghorm**: always moving, always circling the Core, plowing through everything. You'll cross its 8-tile-wide track eventually no matter which direction you explore. Ground slimes spawn along the track — clear the track first, then damage Ghorm between its passes. Iron gear recommended.

**Boss 3 — Hive Mother**: inside the Larva Hive dungeon in the Clay Caves. Craft the **Hive Mother Scanner** from Glurch's effigy to reveal its exact position. Kill adds first; Iron gear recommended.

## The outer-ring titans

| Boss | Location | Summon | Drops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malugaz the Corrupted | Forgotten Ruins (optional) | Corrupted Core | Malugaz's Skull, Corrupted Chest |
| Azeos the Sky Titan | Azeos' Wilderness arena | Azeos Summoning Idol | Azeos' Claw (100%), Sky Chest, Azeos' Feather (rare) |
| Omoroth the Sea Titan | Sunken Sea | Omoroth Summoning Idol | Required progression drops, Sea Chest |

After the Core activates, follow the Core's hint to the Great Wall gates and into the outer biomes. Azeos is a flying arena boss — bring ranged options; Omoroth fights in water, so movement is everything.

## Multiplayer scaling

Boss health and characteristics scale with the number of connected players (each extra player adds less than the last). Enemy health doesn't scale — but more enemies spawn around each player over time. In **Hard mode**, all enemies and bosses have doubled health and damage, with 50% more loot slots in boss chests as the only reward.

## Progression drops checklist

- Glurch Eye → Glurch effigy
- Ghorm's Horn → Ghorm effigy
- Heart of the Hive Mother → Hive Mother effigy
- All three active → **Core powers up**

## FAQ

**How do I beat Glurch?** Tin gear or better, keep distance and dodge its jumps. Drop a bed nearby first so death isn't a long run back.

**Where is the Hive Mother?** In the Larva Hive, a sub-biome of the Clay Caves ~330 tiles from the Core. Use the Hive Mother Scanner (crafted at Glurch's effigy) to locate it.

**What is the boss order in Core Keeper?** Glurch → Ghorm → Hive Mother → Core activation → Malugaz (optional) → Azeos → Omoroth.
