Core Keeper's world is generated in **rings around the Core**, and each ring holds different ores. This Core Keeper ore guide maps every ore to the pickaxe tier and biome you need — so you never waste time mining with the wrong tool.

## Pickaxe tiers at a glance

| Pickaxe | Workbench | Materials | Mines |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood Pickaxe | Starting inventory | Wood | Soft dirt walls only |
| Copper Pickaxe | Basic Workbench | 8 Copper Bar, 10 Wood | Copper, Tin |
| Tin Pickaxe | Basic Workbench | 8 Tin Bar, 10 Wood | Iron |
| Iron Pickaxe | Iron Anvil | 8 Iron Bar, 10 Wood | Gold, Scarlet |
| Gold Pickaxe | Gold Anvil | 8 Gold Bar, 10 Wood | Harder ores, faster |
| Scarlet Pickaxe | Scarlet Workbench | 8 Scarlet Bar, 10 Wood | Best pre-endgame speed |

You can technically mine anywhere from the start — but stronger walls block exploration until your pickaxe tier catches up.

## Where each ore spawns (ring biomes)

Core Keeper structures the world in concentric bands around the Core (0–140, 150–450, 450–520, 520–1200+ tiles). Each band holds specific resources:

| Ring | Biome | Key ores & resources | Danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Center | The Undergrounds (0–140) | Copper, Tin, Wood, Stone | Low |
| Inner ring | The Clay Caves (150–450) | **Tin**, Clay, Iron, Larva Hive entrance | Medium |
| Inner ring | The Forgotten Ruins (150–450) | **Iron**, Stone, Scarlet, Ruins chests | Medium |
| Gate ring | The Great Wall (450–520) | Barrier — no resources | High |
| Outer ring | Azeos' Wilderness (520–1200) | **Gold**, Mold sub-biome, Azeos arena | High |
| Outer ring | The Sunken Sea (520–1200) | Sea resources, Omoroth arena | High |

The inner and outer biomes are placed at **seed-dependent angles** around the Core — which direction you find Clay Caves vs Forgotten Ruins varies per world. That's exactly what the [Seed Finder](/tools/seed-finder/) is for: pick a seed where the biomes you need are close.

## Efficient mining tips

- **Smelt in parallel**: you can't speed up one furnace, but several furnaces process ore concurrently.
- **Follow the glitter**: rising glitter marks rooms and plants; large static sparkles mark ore veins in walls.
- **Repair cheaply**: craft low-tier equipment and salvage it for scrap parts — a cheap repair source early on.
- **Gold drops early**: you can find Gold Ore while mining Clay Caves and Forgotten Ruins, before you can use it — stockpile it for Tier 3.

## FAQ

**Where is tin ore in Core Keeper?** Tin is in The Clay Caves (inner ring, 150–450 tiles). If you hit Forgotten Ruins first, go the opposite direction from the Core.

**What pickaxe do I need for iron?** A Tin Pickaxe. Iron is found in The Forgotten Ruins.

**Can I mine gold with an iron pickaxe?** Yes — the Iron Pickaxe mines gold and scarlet ore.
