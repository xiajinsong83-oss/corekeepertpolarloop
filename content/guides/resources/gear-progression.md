Gear in Core Keeper follows the same ring-biome ore ladder as mining: copper → tin/bronze → iron → scarlet → octarine → galaxite → solarite. This gear progression guide maps each tier to its armor set, the weapon worth crafting, and the accessory slots that actually move your build — so you stop wasting bars on upgrades you'll wear for ten minutes.

## Armor sets by tier

Every armor piece breaks down the same way: a helm, a chest and pants, each with its own durability. Mixing pieces from different sets is fine, but the 3-piece set bonus is where the top tiers pay off. Base-level stats from the official wiki:

| Set | Base lvl | Helm | Chest | Pants | 3-piece set bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copper | 3 | +8 HP · +4 armor | +10 HP · +7 armor | +9 HP · +6 armor | — |
| Bronze | 5 | +12 HP · +6 armor · +5% mining damage | +18 HP · +9 armor · +7.4% melee damage | +14 HP · +8 armor · +6% range damage | — |
| Iron | 7 | +15 HP · +10 armor · +4.8% damage | +25 HP · +12 armor · +7.2% damage | +20 HP · +12 armor · +5.8% damage | +19% armor while at low health |
| Scarlet | 10 | +25 HP · +12 armor · +4% crit chance | +31 HP · +19 armor · +6% crit chance | +27 HP · +16 armor · +5% crit chance | Killing an enemy grants +3% crit chance, stacks up to 5 times |
| Octarine | 12 | +29 HP · +15 armor · +6% mining speed | +37 HP · +22 armor · +7.5% melee attack speed | +32 HP · +18 armor · +6.4% range attack speed | Attack speed +0.2% for every 1% of missing health |
| Galaxite | 14 | +34 HP · +18 armor · +14% crit damage | +42 HP · +26 armor · +19% crit damage | +43 HP · +20 armor · +19% crit damage | +12.7% damage while at max health |
| Solarite | 16 | +36 HP · +20 armor · +14% crit damage · +4 HP on melee hit | +52 HP · +26 armor · +7.8% atk speed · +6 HP on melee hit | +51 HP · +27 armor · +7% crit · +5 HP on melee hit | +23.6% damage while at max health |

Two patterns worth noticing: the **Iron set rewards low health** (tanks who fight on the edge), while **Galaxite and Solarite reward staying topped off** — which pairs naturally with a Heart Berry regen build.

## Craft cost cheat sheet

Recipes are gated behind the matching anvil (Copper/Tin/Iron/Scarlet/Octarine/Galaxite/Solarite Anvil). Approximate bar spend for a full 3-piece set:

| Set | Crafting station | Rough materials per full set |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | Copper Anvil | ~21 Copper Bar (6+8+7) |
| Bronze | Tin Anvil | ~10 Tin Bar + 10 Copper Bar + ~8 Fiber |
| Iron | Iron Anvil | ~38 Iron Bar + ~6 Gold Bar + ~17 Fiber |
| Scarlet | Scarlet Anvil | ~47 Scarlet Bar + ~9 Iron Bar + ~12 Wool |
| Octarine | Octarine Anvil | ~60+ Octarine Bar + ~13 Scarlet Bar + ~11 Gold Bar |
| Galaxite | Galaxite Anvil | ~84 Galaxite Bar + ~30 Octarine Bar + ~12 Strolly Poly Plate |
| Solarite | Solarite Anvil | ~100 Solarite Bar + ~33 Gold Bar + ~18 Strolly Poly Plate |

Gold Bar shows up inside mid-tier recipes from Iron onward — that's why stockpiling gold ore early pays off, even before you can smelt it in a Smelter Kiln.

## Weapons: what to actually craft

You don't need one of everything. Match the weapon to the tier gate you're facing:

| Tier | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Wooden Sword (lvl 1, 30–36 dmg) | What you wake up with — fine for The Undergrounds |
| Early | Tin Sword (lvl 5, 89–107 dmg) | Doubles your damage before the first boss |
| Mid | Iron Sword (8 Iron Bar + 1 Gold Bar) or Iron Bow (lvl 7, 100–122 dmg) | Bow adds +17.9% range damage and Strong Multishot secondary |
| Magic | Fireball Staff (lvl 8, 187–227 dmg) | +35 burn on hit, Explosion Projectile secondary |
| Late melee | Scarlet Sword (lvl 9, 147–179 dmg, +8% crit chance) | Solid all-rounder into the Sunken Sea |
| Crit dagger build | Galaxite Dagger (lvl 14, 189–231 dmg, +14% crit chance) | 3.3 attacks/sec turns crit chance into sustained DPS |

Daggers trade raw damage for attack speed — they synergize with crit-chance accessories and the Octarine "faster when hurt" set bonus.

## Accessories that change your build

Accessories sit outside the armor set system, so you can slot them into any tier. Cheap wins first:

| Slot | Pick | Effect | When to grab |
|---|---|---|---|
| Necklace | Copper Cross Necklace | +4% critical hit chance | First hour |
| Necklace | Iron Chunk Necklace | +12 armor | Iron tier |
| Necklace | Gold Crystal Necklace | +8% magic damage · +8.3% minion damage | Minion/magic builds |
| Necklace | Octarine Necklace | +5.6% melee and range attack speed | Octarine tier |
| Lantern | Small Lantern → Lantern → Pearl Lantern | +3 → +4 → +5 blue glow (Pearl adds +7% move speed, +1.6 mana/s) | Upgrade as you dive deeper |
| Off-hand | Wooden Shield → Iron Shield | Block reduces incoming damage by 70% while held | Any tier — shields are the safest second slot |
| Ring | Lucky Ring | +7% chance at extra ore from ore walls · +7% better hook on fishing | Late-game QoL |

Glow is not cosmetic: it reveals deeper, darker biomes, so don't sleep on lantern upgrades when you push into the Sunken Sea.

## Reinforcement: the hidden power spike

Reinforcing gear at the **Salvage and Repair Station** (rather than just repairing it) gives every reinforced item **+15% damage and stats** — attack speed is deliberately untouched — and doubles its durability pool. The bonus lasts until durability drops back to half, then fades and can be reapplied. Practically: reinforce your main weapon and one spare set right before a boss, not the moment you craft them, so the buff covers the fight.

## FAQ

**When should I upgrade from iron to scarlet gear?** Once you can mine scarlet ore in the Forgotten Ruins and Azeos' Wilderness and have a Scarlet Workbench online — the Scarlet set jumps you from level 7 to level 10.

**Does armor set bonus matter?** Yes. The 3-piece set bonus (Iron, Scarlet, Octarine, Galaxite, Solarite) often outperforms mixing in a stronger single piece — wear the full set before splashing.

**What does reinforcement do?** At the Salvage and Repair Station, reinforcement boosts an item's damage and stats by 15% (attack speed unchanged) and doubles durability until it halves back to base.
