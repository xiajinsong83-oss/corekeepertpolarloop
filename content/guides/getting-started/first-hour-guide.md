The Core Keeper **getting started** experience is built around one loop: craft a workbench, make stronger tools, mine tougher walls, find better ores, and repeat. This guide walks through world creation, the first hour, and the three early bosses you need to beat before the Core powers up.

## World creation: what to pick

Select **Start Game** from the main menu to create a world. You can also join a friend's world from the Steam friends list — Core Keeper has native multiplayer with no dedicated server required, and more than the officially stated 8 players can connect.

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Difficulty: Normal | Standard enemy and boss health |
| Difficulty: Hard | All enemies and bosses have **doubled base health and damage**; 50% more loot slots in boss chests; no unique hard-mode items |
| World name / icon | Cannot be changed in-game afterwards |

Your character and world saves are stored separately, so one character can visit multiple worlds. Pick a character background — each gives a small starting loadout:

| Background | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Chef | +3 Cooking, 1 Cooking Pot, 8 Mushroom |
| Gardener | +3 Gardening, 1 Copper Hoe, 1 Watering Can |
| Fisherman | +3 Fishing, 1 Wood Fishing Rod, 3 Food Ration |
| Nomad | No items or skill bonus |

On death, a Normal character drops inventory into a gravestone and respawns at the Core or your bed; a Hardcore character loses everything and the save is deleted.

## The wood-to-iron tool loop

You spawn in front of **the Core**, an ancient machine surrounded by roots. Break the roots for **Wood**, then craft four basics from your inventory: Torch, Wood Pickaxe, Wood Shovel and the Basic Workbench.

The upgrade loop repeats all game:

1. Craft a workbench
2. Use the workbench to craft stronger tools
3. Use stronger tools to mine tougher walls for stronger resources
4. Use those resources to craft a stronger workbench — repeat

**Tier 1 — Copper**: Wood Pickaxe → mine **Copper Ore** in The Undergrounds. Look for rising glitter (rooms, plants) and large static sparkles (ore veins in walls). Smelt copper in a Furnace, craft Copper Pickaxe + tools at the Basic Workbench.

**Tier 2 — Tin**: Copper tools → cross the chasm (build a Wood Bridge) into **The Clay Caves**. If you find The Forgotten Ruins first, walk the opposite direction from the Core. Mine **Tin Ore**, craft the Tin Workbench.

**Tier 3 — Iron**: Head to **The Forgotten Ruins** for **Iron Ore** (you may already have **Gold Ore** from Clay Caves / Ruins). Craft the Iron Workbench, then it's boss time.

## The three early bosses

You must defeat three bosses to activate the three effigies next to the Core. Each drops a required item:

| Boss | Location from Core | Drops | Recommended gear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glurch the Abominable Mass | ~65 tiles, random angle | Glurch Eye | Tin or better |
| Ghorm the Devourer | ~250 tiles, constantly circles the Core on an 8-tile-wide track | Ghorm's Horn | Iron |
| Hive Mother | ~330 tiles, in the Larva Hive (sub-biome of Clay Caves) | Heart of the Hive Mother | Iron |

Tip: craft the **Hive Mother Scanner** after activating Glurch's effigy to locate the hive. After defeating each boss, place its drop on the matching effigy — each activated effigy becomes a workbench with new recipes. Activate all three and the Core powers up, opening the rest of the underground.

## Quick survival tips

- **Torches**: hold Shift to auto-equip a torch while exploring.
- **Smelting speed**: you can't speed up a single furnace, but you can build several furnaces and process ores in parallel.
- **Bed before boss**: sleep in a bed near the arena before fighting — you'll respawn there if you die.
- **Hunger** doesn't kill you: it only reduces max health and stats.
- **NPCs move in** when a room is 2×2 to 8×8 tiles, has a door, a bed and the NPC's required item, and no players are within 30 tiles for about a minute.

## FAQ

**Can I change my world's difficulty later?** No — name, icon and difficulty can't be modified in-game.

**How many players can join a world?** Native multiplayer supports more than the official 8-player limit; boss health scales with connected players (with diminishing returns per additional player).

**What do I craft first?** Torch, Wood Pickaxe, Wood Shovel, then the Basic Workbench.
