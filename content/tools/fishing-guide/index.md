## How fishing works

The water tile's **type** (normal, yellow, moldy, seawater, lava, shimmering, grimy) determines the loot table, and your **fishing power** must exceed the water's requirement to catch anything at all. Higher fishing power also speeds up catches. Casting into fish splashes guarantees a fish (and a reeling mini-game); non-fish catches are pulled instantly.

## FAQ

**Where do I catch a Mold Shark?** Fish in moldy water in the Mold biome — it's a 50% catch.

**Can I fish in lava?** Yes — the Molten Quarry uses lava tables with Lava Eaters and Dragonfish.

**What are the rarest fish?** Starlight Nautilus (6.1% in lava), Cosmic Form (11.1% shimmering) and Riftian Lampfish (9.5% in The Passage).
