The Core Keeper **item crafting calculator** expands any recipe into its full material list. Pick a target item and a quantity, and it multiplies every ingredient automatically — perfect for planning a big crafting run.

## How to use

1. Choose the item you want to craft.
2. Set the target quantity.
3. Read the multiplied material list and the workbench you need.

## FAQ

- **Does it include all recipes?** Recipe data is synced from the community wiki and updated after each major patch.
- **Can I plan bulk crafting?** Yes — set a target quantity and the ingredient list scales automatically.
- **Which workbench do I need?** Each recipe lists its station (Basic Workbench, Iron Anvil, etc.).
