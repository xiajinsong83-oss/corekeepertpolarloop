The Core Keeper **base layout planner** collects blueprint references for building efficient bases — functional zoning, automated production lines and circuit wiring. Use the diagrams as a starting point, then adapt to your seed and resources.

## Blueprint categories

- **Early base** — compact starter shelter near the Core
- **Farm layout** — crop plots, sprinklers and food production
- **Automation** — conveyor loops, drills and sorting lines
- **Defense** — walls, traps and boss-arena prep

## FAQ

- **Can I submit my own base?** Blueprint submissions are welcome via the contact email.
- **Are layouts version-proof?** New biomes and items may require layout tweaks after updates.
- **Do blueprints depend on the seed?** Resource positions vary by seed, so always scout before building.
