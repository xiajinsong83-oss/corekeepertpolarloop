## FAQ

- **Are drop chances exact?** Chances come from the community wiki and may change with patches.
- **Which bosses are optional?** Malugaz, Morpha, Igneous and others have separate optional-boss entries.
- **What does each boss drop for progression?** Main bosses drop their unique item (e.g. Glurch Eye, Ghorm's Horn) required to progress.
