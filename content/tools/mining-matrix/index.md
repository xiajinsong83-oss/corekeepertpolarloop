## Pickaxe progression

Tools scale from the hand-crafted Wood Pickaxe (15 mining damage) through Copper, Tin, Iron, Scarlet, Octarine, Galaxite and Solarite tiers to the Soul Seeker (769, unbreakable). Durability jumps from 200 to 800 at Iron tier and stays there.

## FAQ

**Which pickaxe do I need for stone?** Stone blocks need 67+ mining damage — a Tin Pickaxe (83) is the first tool that works.

**When do I get Iron?** The Iron Pickaxe (167) unlocks grass and scarlet-grade walls (147+), and is the standard mid-game tool.

**Is the Ancient Pickaxe worth it?** It's a strong early skip dropped by Azeos the Sky Titan — 354 damage at the point you'd otherwise still be on Scarlet.
