## Achievement categories

**Boss achievements** cover all main and optional bosses from Glurch to the 1.3 titans. **Skill achievements** require 100 points in each of the 10 skills. The rest mix progress milestones (recipes, golden plants, pets) with hidden challenges such as playing the Tune of Tempest.

## FAQ

**Are achievements shared between worlds?** They are account-wide in Core Keeper, so your checklist stays relevant across saves.

**What is the Tune of Tempest?** Play the notes **XVW XVW** on any instrument to unlock "A Strange Song".

**How do I get over 200% movement speed?** Stack sprint bonuses, movement food buffs and accessories — the "In a Rush" hidden achievement rewards the build.
