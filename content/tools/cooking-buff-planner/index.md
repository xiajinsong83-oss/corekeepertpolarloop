## How food buffs work in Core Keeper

When you eat food, each effect has a **duration and a value**. The key rule to remember:

- **Same-type buffs do not stack** — eating two foods that both give movement speed only applies the higher value.
- **Different buff types combine freely** — a max-health dish plus a damage dish works together.
- **Effects tick on a timer** — most dishes last 10 minutes, drinks like Cave Coffee and Kizz Cola last 20–60 seconds, so time your consumption for boss fights.

## FAQ

**Can I stack two different damage foods?** No — melee, range and magic are each separate buff types, but two melee-damage dishes will only give you the higher one.

**How long do buffs last?** Food effects run 10 minutes; drinks are short-lived (20–60 seconds). Potions run 1 minute.

**What is the best food before a boss?** Glazed Heart Berry (max health) + a damage dish matching your build + a drink like Kizz Cola for crit. The planner above shows every option per buff.
