## How cooked dishes combine

Each ingredient carries hidden cooked effects. When you combine two ingredients in a Cooking Pot, **permanent max-health boosts stack**, while every other effect keeps only the **highest value** of the two. Cooking skills (Utilizing every nutrient, Fast food, Long-lasting food) then boost the result retroactively.

## FAQ

**What gives the most food?** Bloat Oat is the raw food king at +43 food; Golden variants trade a little food for big buffs.

**Best boss-fighting food?** Golden Puffungi cuts boss damage taken by 9% and adds +14% boss damage — plus regen.

**Melee or magic buffs?** Pinegrapple/Golden Pinegrapple for melee, Sunrice for magic damage, Lunacorn for minions, Pewpaya for ranged.
