**The Great Egg Hunt** is Core Keeper's Easter seasonal event — and the very first seasonal event the game ever ran, originally held April 13–19. It now recurs every spring, with a default window of roughly **Apr 5 – Apr 16**. The idea is simple: dig through every biome, collect coloured eggs dropped by enemies and destructible objects, then spend them at the [Seasonal Merchant](/wiki/seasonal-merchant/) and the event-only **Easter Workbench**. The prize at the end is a permanent collectible — the Large Golden Egg — which doubles as the summon item for an optional remixed boss, **Awakened Azeos**.

For the broader calendar of celebrations, see [Seasonal Events](/wiki/seasonal/); for how the Easter Workbench compares with the Festive, Samhain and Valentine benches, see [Seasonal Workbenches](/wiki/seasonal-workbenches/).

## The four coloured eggs

Easter eggs drop as loot from enemies and destructible objects, and the colour you find depends on which biome you are hunting in. **Once the event ends, eggs stop dropping** — but every egg and egg-item you already own stays in your storage and keeps working forever.

| Egg | Where it drops |
|---|---|
| Brown Easter Egg | Enemies and destructible objects in The Undergrounds |
| Rose Easter Egg | Enemies and destructible objects in The Clay Caves |
| Gray Easter Egg | Enemies and destructible objects in The Forgotten Ruins and the Mold Biome |
| Green Easter Egg | Enemies and destructible objects in Azeos' Wilderness and the Mold Biome |

Practical takeaway: you need a balanced pile of all four colours. The Mold Biome covers both Gray and Green eggs, so it is the most efficient single hunting ground once you have unlocked it.

## The Large Golden Egg

The end-of-hunt trophy is bought from the **Seasonal Merchant**, not crafted:

| Result | Materials |
|---|---|
| Large Golden Egg | 5 Brown + 5 Rose + 5 Gray + 5 Green Easter Eggs |

It is a permanent decorative showpiece for your base — and, as the wiki puts it, "legends tell of another secret use for the Golden Egg". That secret use is the boss summon below.

## Awakened Azeos — the Golden Egg's secret use

Placing the **Large Golden Egg** on Azeos' arena summons **Awakened Azeos**. Mechanically it is identical to Azeos the Sky Titan apart from its colour and its loot table, and it drops **all of its own notable gear on top of the original Azeos drops**, with the new items guaranteed.

| Drop | Type | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| Golden Titan's Chest | Storage | 36 storage slots |
| Awakened Azeos Figurine | Collectible figurine | Display piece for your base |
| Radical Rabbit Ears | Helm | +23 max health, +3.8% movement speed, +6% dodge chance |
| Radical Rabbit Headband | Helm | +23 max health, +3.8% movement speed, +6% dodge chance |
| Eostre Necklace | Necklace | +10.3% extra harvest chance, +4.9% movement speed |
| Ostara Ring | Ring | +7% dodge chance, +0.9 health per second |

The **three-piece set bonus** (mix and match across Radical Rabbit Ears, Radical Rabbit Headband, Eostre Necklace and Ostara Ring) grants **+7% melee and range attack speed**. Note the two helms are interchangeable set pieces, so you only need one of them plus the necklace and the ring.

## FAQ

**When does The Great Egg Hunt run?** The recurring window is roughly Apr 5 – Apr 16. It was first held Apr 13–19 at launch. Outside the window, force it with the season override (`-season Easter` on a server, or Settings → Gameplay).

**Do eggs expire when the event ends?** No. They stop dropping, but every egg you collected stays in your inventory and still trades at the merchant when the next Easter rolls around.

**Which biome should I farm?** The Mold Biome drops both Gray and Green eggs, making it the most colour-efficient spot. Otherwise split your time across the four biome families.

**Do I have to fight Awakened Azeos during the event?** No. The Large Golden Egg you buy is permanent — you can summon Awakened Azeos whenever you like, even off-season, once you own the egg.

**Is Awakened Azeos harder than normal Azeos?** The wiki states it is identical apart from colour and drops. Expect the same fight you already know, with a better loot table.

**What is the Golden Titan's Chest?** A 36-slot storage chest themed after the boss — a guaranteed drop from Awakened Azeos and a rare decorative storage option.
