**Accessories** are the jewelry and bag slots that sit outside your armor set: a **Necklace**, a **Ring** and a **Backpack** slot. They are where builds actually get defined — armor gives stats, accessories give *thematic effects*: minion builds, fishing-as-range-damage, explosive crits, mana-barrier tanking, and pet buffs all live in jewelry. Most are crafted at the **Jewelry Workbench** (Iron Workbench tier) or the upgraded **Proficient Jewelry Workbench**, with polished versions available on early and mid-tier pieces.

*Source: [Core Keeper Wiki — Accessories](https://corekeeper.atma.gg/en/Accessories).*

## How polished variants work

Several early jewelry recipes roll a chance on craft to come out as a **Polished** variant — same icon, stronger numbers, and a higher item level. The crafting skill perk **Jewelry Crafter** boosts this chance from **+10% to +50%** per perk point, so once you are farming jewelry in bulk the perk pays for itself.

| Base necklace | Polished upgrade | Example stats jump |
|---|---|---|
| Copper Cross Necklace | Polished Copper Cross Necklace | +4% crit → +6% crit +18% crit damage |
| Iron Chunk Necklace | Polished Iron Chunk Necklace | +12 armor → +20 armor +38 max health |
| Gold Crystal Necklace | Polished Gold Crystal Necklace | +8% magic +8.3% minion → +19% magic +20.1% minion |
| Octarine Necklace | Polished Octarine Necklace | +5.6% atk speed → +7.5% atk speed +5.5% move speed |
| Scarlet Chunk Necklace | Polished Scarlet Chunk Necklace | +19 armor → +26 armor +9% dodge |
| Coral Amulet | Polished Coral Amulet | +11% boat speed → +17% boat speed +12% better fish bites |

## Which jewelry to craft first

- **Early game (Copper → Iron):** Polished Copper Cross Necklace for crit chance; Iron Chunk Necklace for raw armor.
- **Mid game (Scarlet → Octarine):** commit to a build — Gold Crystal Necklace for minion/magic, Octarine Necklace for attack speed, or start the Neptune trio if you fish.
- **Late game:** the cross-piece two-piece bonuses (Moonbell, Warped, Pyrdra/Druidra/Crydra) outscale raw stat sticks. Ancient Guardian pieces are the default boss-fight swap.
- **Minion builds:** stack Soul Medallion + Jailor's Ring + Hourglass Ring together — they are the only sources of extra minion count and mana reservation reduction.

## FAQ

**Do I have to craft both pieces of a set myself?** No. Set bonuses trigger as long as both pieces are equipped, regardless of how you obtained them (crafting, drops, seasonal events).

**What does the Jewelry Crafter perk actually do?** It raises the chance that a craft rolls the Polished variant, from +10% at one point up to +50% at five. It does not change non-jewelry recipes.

**Can I wear two rings?** Only one ring slot is available by default. Extra ring slots are not unlocked through jewelry itself — check game updates for any slot-expansion features.

**Do accessory bonuses stack with armor set bonuses?** Yes. Accessories and armor are separate buckets; a 2-set Sentry armor bonus and a 2-set Moonbell accessory bonus both apply independently.

**Where do seasonal accessories like Valentine or Ostara come from?** They are event-only crafts from seasonal workbenches. See [Seasonal Workbenches](/wiki/seasonal-workbenches/) and [Seasonal Events](/wiki/seasonal/).
