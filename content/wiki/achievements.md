**Achievements** in Core Keeper are the Steam trophy trackers that shadow your run: roughly sixty trophies split between boss kills, skill masteries, rare collectibles and a handful of deliberately silly hidden tasks. Many show their condition up front, while the hidden ones stay blank until you stumble into them.

## How the list is structured

- Visible trophies hand you the objective in the Steam client; hidden trophies show only a name and an icon, so spoilers are built into the design.
- Most progress toward completion happens naturally as you explore: beating new bosses, raising skills, and collecting signature weapons ticks off a large chunk of the list without farming.
- The long pole is the set of "gain 100 skill points" trophies, which reward extended play in a particular combat or gathering loop rather than a single action.

## Quirky and world trophies

The rest of the list rewards experimentation rather than progress. Highlights:

- Legendary Fossil — catch a Starlight Nautilus while fishing (hidden)
- In a Rush — push your movement speed past 200% (hidden)
- Gossip Group — talk to a Big Larva while wearing the Larva Helm and Larva Chest
- A Throne Fit for a King — sit on a Caveling Toilet while wearing a crown (hidden)
- Rock and Stone! — equip both the Ring of Rock and the Ring of Stone
- Impersonator Syndrome — dress like a character from the official illustrations (hidden)
- A Strange Song — play the note sequence "XVW XVW" on any instrument
- Beam Me Up — use a Waypoint for the first time
- Your Very First — hatch a pet from an egg
- Pet Prodigy — raise a pet to max level; I want 'em all! — store every color variant of one pet type in a chest
- Legion Commander — keep eight or more minions active at once
- Giddy Up — mount cattle with a Saddle or Racing Saddle; A Good Life — feed cattle
- Fortune and Glory — wear the full Explorer's vanity set with a Hookshot equipped
- Racer's Garage — store the Primitive, Renegade and Speeder Go-Karts in one chest
- 5 Centimeters Per Second — plant ten Cherry Trees (available only during the Cherry Blossom seasonal event)

## Completion tips and roadmap

- Knock out the visible boss trophies in order as you push progression; they unlock automatically and feed your weapon and armor upgrades.
- Treat the level-100 skill trophies as a long-haul background task rather than a grind. Build your base, cook everything you discover and fish on the side so several masteries rise together.
- Hidden trophies are meant to be discovered — wander, wear odd gear combinations and talk to NPCs in costume instead of looking up spoilers.
- For gear-locked tasks, keep the required items on hand: Larva armor for Gossip Group, a crown for the toilet trophy, and the Explorer set plus a Hookshot for Fortune and Glory.
- Seasonal trophies like the Cherry Trees one are time-gated, so grab them whenever the matching event is live.
- Cook every new recipe as you unlock ingredients; Certified Chef at 100 recipes is far easier if you never skip a cook.
