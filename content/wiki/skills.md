**Skills** are your character's passive progression track. Rather than spending points by hand, you earn experience simply by doing the activities the game already expects — swinging a pickaxe, swinging a sword, running around, planting seeds — and that experience slowly fills each skill bar toward level 100. Every discipline grants a small permanent bonus as it levels, and every fifth level hands you a talent point to spend in that skill's own tree. Because respecs are cheap, you can experiment freely instead of locking into one build forever.

## How levels and talent points work

A few rules govern the whole system:

- Experience accrues passively from matching actions until a level-up; each successive level costs noticeably more than the last, and the curve differs between skills.
- Reaching level 5, 10, 15 and so on awards one talent point. At the level 100 cap you also bank a small reserve of extra points.
- Talent points are tied to the skill that earned them — a Mining point cannot be spent in the Melee tree.
- You can refund and re-spend all allocated points at any time for a flat cost of 200 metal bars taken from your inventory, so builds are not permanent commitments.

## The twelve skills at a glance

Each discipline feeds a different stat, and the way you earn its experience differs just as much.

| Skill | How you earn it | Per-level bonus (at level 100) |
|---|---|---|
| Mining | Hitting walls with a pickaxe or fist | +1 mining damage, up to +100 |
| Running | Walking on foot (not minecart, boat or conveyor) | +0.1% move speed, up to +10% |
| Melee combat | Landing a melee hit on an enemy | +0.5% melee damage, up to +50% |
| Vitality | Killing an enemy | +1 max health, up to +100 HP |
| Crafting | Completing a craft | +0.5% armor, up to +50% |
| Range combat | Landing a ranged hit | +0.5% ranged damage, up to +50% |
| Gardening | Hand-harvesting a fully grown plant | +0.4% bonus harvest chance, up to +40% |
| Fishing | Any catch, fish or junk | +1 fishing power, up to +100 |
| Cooking | Taking a dish from the cooking pot | +0.2% extra food chance, up to +20% |
| Magic | Hitting an enemy with a magic weapon | +0.5% magic damage, up to +50% |
| Summoning | Your minion striking an enemy | +0.5% minion damage, up to +50% |
| Explosives | An enemy taking blast damage from you | +0.5% blast damage, up to +50% |

## Choosing a build: mining, combat and farming

Your play style decides which talent trees to invest in first.

- **Mining build:** take Efficient excavation for raw mining damage, Meticulous miner for extra ore per vein, and Mine, mine, mine! for swing speed. Self-mending alloy keeps your pickaxe in the ground longer, while Archaeologist adds a small chance of bonus loot from any wall you break.
- **Combat build:** Melee players want Quick strikes and Building anger for sustained damage, plus Strength of the Ancients for a boss damage spike. Ranged and magic equivalents mirror this with Rapid shots, Focused accuracy, True sight and Arcane frenzy. Pair either with Vitality's Cheat death and Protection of the Ancients for survivability.
- **Farming build:** Gardening's Grateful gardener restores your seed return, Expert gardener can grow golden plants, and Cooking's Healthy diet plus Long-lasting food make your meal buffs both stronger and longer. Fishing sits nicely here too, since Chewy bait and Fisherman's luck stretch your bait supply.

## Levelling faster — and common mistakes

- **Hit, don't kill.** Melee, ranged and magic experience is granted per landing, not per kill, so repeatedly tapping weak enemies levels you faster than finishing fights.
- **Bulk-craft on purpose.** Crafting XP is awarded per craft action, and holding Ctrl to craft ten at once counts as ten separate crafts — great for pushing Crafting.
- **Punch for mining XP.** Walls that break too slowly give no experience, but deliberately punching weak walls (without a pickaxe) farms steady XP; just make sure your mining stat clears the wall's reduction threshold by 12 or more.
- **Move on foot.** Riding a minecart or boat gives zero Running experience, so walk when you can.
- **Don't hoard points.** Because refunds cost only 200 bars, sitting on unspent talent points wastes potential — spend them, and respec later if a tree underperforms.
- **Specialise early, balance later.** Pushing one or two core skills to 50+ before spreading out gives a smoother early-mid game than levelling all twelve evenly.
