**Gardening** lets you turn loose seeds into a repeatable supply of food, fiber and crafting materials. You till a patch of suitable dirt, plant a seed, keep it watered, and pick the mature crop by hand. Nearly every harvest also feeds the cooking loop, so a well-kept farm is the backbone of long-term survivability in the underground caves.

## How the farming loop works

A typical farm follows the same short cycle every run:

- **Till the ground** with a hoe on a block type the crop will accept.
- **Plant the seed** on the tilled soil.
- **Water it** once with a Watering Can, or let a Sprinkler handle it automatically.
- **Wait through the growth stages** (see timing below).
- **Harvest by hand** — manual picking is what grants gardening experience and may hand back a replacement seed.

One quirk catches newcomers: wood crops (Root Seed) do **not** give gardening XP when picked. They spread wood around themselves as they mature, which is useful for building but won't level your skill.

## Growth timing

Once a seed has been watered, the whole cycle takes ten real minutes before it is ready to pick. The clock splits into three visible stages:

| Stage transition | Time elapsed |
|---|---|
| Watered seed → sprout | 2 minutes |
| Sprout → pre-harvest | 4 minutes |
| Pre-harvest → ready-to-harvest crop | 4 minutes |
| **Total from water to harvest** | **10 minutes** |

Because the timer runs on real time rather than play time, you can plant a batch, head out to explore a biome, and walk back to a fully grown field without burning extra in-game effort.

| Glow Tulip Seed | Glow Tulip (and Golden Glow Tulip) | Standard tilled soil |

Most non-wood crops have a small base chance (3%) to sprout as a **Golden** variant — a stronger ingredient that matters a great deal when you cook with it.

## Leveling, seeds and harvest odds

Each crop you pick by hand gives one gardening experience point. Two mechanics shape how productive your farm feels:

- **Seed return**: early in the run, roughly one in four harvests fails to hand back a seed, so you burn through seed stock fast. This stops being a problem once you put 5 points into the **Grateful Gardener** talent, which adds +5% to +25% chance of recovering a seed on harvest.
- **Extra harvest chance**: every gardening level adds +0.4% chance of pulling bonus produce, reaching +40% at level 100. In practice that means a high-level farmer averages about 14 vegetables from every 10 plants.

Key gardening talents worth grabbing:

- **Grateful Gardener** — seed recovery (your first priority pick).
- **Expert Gardener** — +3% to +15% chance that your own plants turn golden.
- **Eat Your Vegetables!** — +5% to +25% food from eating plants or plant-based cooked dishes.
- **Feast for the Eyes** — +1 to +5 extra food per harvest.
- The combat branch (Thorny Weapons, Thorny Skin, Poison Coated Weapons, Potent Poison) turns gardening into a damage-support skill built around crits and poison.

## Gear that multiplies your yield

Beyond the skill itself, accessories and the Farmer's Hat stack harvest chance on top of the leveling bonus. Harvest chance is what lets you pull **double, and eventually triple, crops** from a single plant:

| Source | Default harvest bonus | At level 19 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gardening skill | 40% at level 100 | — | +0.4% per level |
| Farmer's Hat | 10.1% (+1.5% reinforced) | 20.8% (+3.1% reinforced) | |
| Petal Ring | 22.7% | 42.2% | Two rings stack: 45.4% / 84.4% |
| Eostre Necklace | 10.3% | 19.1% | |
| Kelp Dumpling food | +15% for 10 minutes | — | Consumable buff |

Even with zero gardening levels, wearing all of this gear already gives you 67.3% harvest chance. Once every accessory and the hat are leveled to 19, reinforced, and you eat a Kelp Dumpling, you reach 182.4% — that guarantees two crops per harvest and adds an 82.4% shot at a third.

## What this means for players

- **Start small but start early**: a 5×5 tilled plot of Heart Berries in the first biome pays for itself within a few cycles.
- **Unlock Grateful Gardener first**; without it you'll feel like you're losing seeds forever.
- **Match your farm to your biome**: Carrock needs stone, Puffungi needs mold, the beach trio needs sand blocks, and Sunrice/Lunacorn only take crystal — plan a multi-biome base if you want every crop.
- **Use Sprinklers** rather than the Watering Can once your field grows past a dozen tiles; the time you save watering by hand is enormous.
- **Save Golden crops for cooking**, not raw eating — they push dishes into higher rarity tiers.
