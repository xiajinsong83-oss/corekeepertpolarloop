**Fishing** is a leisure skill in Core Keeper that quietly pays for itself: toss a rod into any water tile, wait for a bite, and pull up fish, ore, kelp, loose tools or even locked chests. It also feeds the cooking loop, because a well-cooked fish dish hands you a real damage buff right before a boss attempt.

## How a catch actually works

- Stand next to any water source and cast the bobber. A one-tile puddle works as well as a vast lake — the size of the connected water body never changes what you hook.
- Watch for fish splashes on the surface. Dropping the bobber onto a splash spot guarantees a fish and triggers the reeling mini-game; keep the moving marker inside the zone to land it, or turn the mini-game off in settings for instant pulls.
- Everything that is not a fish — ore, kelp, scrap parts, valuables, keys and chests — is hauled out immediately with no reeling step.
- A chaotic fallback: lob an explosive into a pool. It still awards fishing experience and occasionally knocks fish loose, though it is far slower than fishing with a rod.

## Fishing power, rods and bait

- Every body of water carries an implicit fishing-power threshold. If your total power sits below that threshold, the line comes back empty; once you clear the bar, any extra power shortens bites and pull time.
- Rod tiers raise your base fishing power as you craft stronger versions, which is what lets you move from the starting dirt ring into deeper, colored-water biomes.
- Bait goes into the off-hand slot and tunes the roll: it raises the chance that the hooked item is a real fish, speeds up bites, and can push the odds toward rarer specimens. Because bait is consumed per cast, keep cheap bait equipped while leveling and save the good bait for targeted rare-fish hunts.

## Leveling up and the skill tree

- Every catch — fish or junk — grants one experience point. Because junk pulls skip the mini-game, reeling garbage is actually the fastest way to grind early levels.
- Each fishing level adds +1 fishing power, and the stat caps out at 100 power once you reach fishing level 100.
- The fishing talent tree mixes bite speed, fish odds and combat perks. The most useful nodes:

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Improved Bait | Fish bite 5–25% faster |
| Fisherman's Luck | +4–20% chance the hooked item is a fish |
| Angler's Advantage | +2–10% chance a rarer fish takes the line |
| Chewy Bait | 5–25% chance the bait survives the catch |
| Well-Trained Aim | 6–30% of your fishing power added as range damage |
| Power of Omega-3! | +3–15% damage against bosses after eating fish or fish dishes |
| Steady Feet | 10–50% less impact from slippery movement |
| Studied Patterns | +2–10% dodge chance |

## Water types and where to fish

- The color of the water decides your loot table more than the biome label on your map. Plain blue water follows the biome you stand in, while colored water follows its own table no matter where you built the pool — yellow water anywhere rolls on the Clay Caves and Larva Hive list.
- The ten fishing zones progress from the starting Dirt ring through the Clay Caves and Larva Hive, the Forgotten Ruins, Azeos' Wilderness, the Mold biome, the Sunken Sea, the Desert of Beginnings, the Molten Quarry, the Shimmering Frontier and The Passage.
- Early-game dirt water gives a simple fish table:

| Dirt-water catch | Approx. rate |
|---|---|
| Orange Cave Guppy | 37% |
| Blue Cave Guppy | 29% |
| Rock Jaw | 21% |
| Gem Crab | 13% |

- The same dirt pools also cough up Green Kelp, Copper Ore, Scrap Parts, Fiber and a few valuables — Copper Key, Locked Copper Chest, Scuba Fins, Golden Starfish, the Cave Guppy Necklace and Amber Fish Egg. Yellow water in the Clay Caves swaps in Yellow Kelp, Tin Ore and Larva Hive drops.

## What fish are actually for

- Cooked fish feed the cooking skill and, once the Omega-3 node is unlocked, give you a window of bonus boss damage — so eating a fish dish before a fight is a real tactical choice, not just flavor.
- One specific catch drives a hidden Steam achievement: haul up a Starlight Nautilus and the Legendary Fossil trophy pops.
- Ore, kelp and chests pulled from the water are free side income while you relax between boss attempts, and the Copper Key / Locked Copper Chest combo is a nice early boost near a fresh base.

## What this means for players

- Level with junk first. Cast at a plain pool near your base and bank every instant pull instead of fighting the mini-game for experience.
- Learn the water colors. If you want Clay Caves loot but are standing in the dirt ring, seek out — or build — a yellow pool rather than reeling generic blue water.
- Aim at visible splashes whenever you genuinely need a fish for a quest, a recipe or the Starlight Nautilus hunt.
- Queue up a fish dish before every major boss once Power of Omega-3! is on your tree.
- Keep cheap bait in the off-hand while leveling and swap to stronger bait only when farming rare biomes; Chewy Bait then makes the good bait last far longer.
