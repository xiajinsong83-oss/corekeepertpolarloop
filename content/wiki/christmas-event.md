The **Christmas Seasonal Event** is Core Keeper's longest and richest celebration, running roughly **Dec 14 – Jan 4** across the new year. While it is live, the world visibly transforms: The Core itself, boss statues and certain other objects become coated in a temporary snow layer that melts away again when the event ends. The title screen logo switches to its frosty variant, a roster of mobs dresses up in holiday costumes, and the [Seasonal Merchant](/wiki/seasonal-merchant/) opens a full festive storefront bought with **Cookies & Milk**.

This page goes deep on the Christmas-specific loop — the currency, the costumed mobs, the Festive Workbench recipes and what is actually inside the presents. For the yearly calendar and the season override, see [Seasonal Events](/wiki/seasonal/); for how the Festive Workbench fits the other event benches, see [Seasonal Workbenches](/wiki/seasonal-workbenches/).

## How the Christmas loop works

1. Hang a **Seasonal Calendar** (crafted at the Carpenter's Table) on the wall of a suitable room — the [Seasonal Merchant](/wiki/seasonal-merchant/) moves in immediately once Christmas is active.
2. Kill the costumed seasonal enemies scattered through the early biomes. They drop **Cookies & Milk** as a separate roll on top of their normal loot.
3. Spend Cookies & Milk on the merchant: grab the **Festive Workbench** first, then the Gingerbread House Chest and the Little Helperling.
4. Farm **Snowball** alongside the currency and craft the full festive set at the Festive Workbench.
5. Feed cooked food to the Little Helperling so it cranks out presents for you while you keep exploring.

## Seasonal Merchant stock at Christmas

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Festive Workbench | 5 Cookies & Milk | Unlocks Christmas decorations and fireworks |
| Gingerbread House Chest | 5 Cookies & Milk | Storage with 18 slots |
| Little Helperling | 10 Cookies & Milk | Companion that crafts presents when fed cooked food |

The **Little Helperling** is the companion twist of Christmas: feed it **Uncommon-or-lower rarity cooked food** and it produces a **Winter Present**; feed it **Rare or higher rarity cooked food** and it produces a **Luxury Winter Present**. Both presents are opened for a random festive item.

## Costumed enemies and their drops

These are reskins of existing mobs in the early biomes, so you can farm them in areas you already know. The seasonal drops are rolled separately from the mob's normal loot — they do not replace it.

| Costume | Base mob | Biome | Seasonal drops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snowhead Slime | Orange Slime | The Undergrounds, The Clay Caves | Snowball 10%, Cookies & Milk 20%, Snowman Hat 2%, Snowhead Slime Figurine 1% |
| Carrot Slime | Red Slime | The Undergrounds, The Clay Caves | Snowball 10%, Cookies & Milk 20%, Snowman Hat 2%, Carrot Slime Figurine 1% |
| Santa's Shrooman | Shrooman | The Undergrounds | Snowball 10%, Cookies & Milk 20%, Festive Hat 2%, Santa's Shrooman Figurine 1% |
| Gingerbreadling | Caveling | The Forgotten Ruins | Snowball 10%, Cookies & Milk 20%, Gingerbread Hood / Top / Pants 1% each, Gingerbreadling Figurine 1% |
| Starwand Shaman | Caveling Shaman | The Forgotten Ruins | Snowball 10%, Cookies & Milk 20%, Gingerbreadman Head 2%, Starwand Shaman Figurine 1% |
| Bakery Brute | Caveling Brute | The Forgotten Ruins | Snowball 10%, Cookies & Milk 20%, Bakery Brute Figurine 1% |

Two practical notes from the table:

- **Cookies & Milk drops at a flat 20%** on every costumed mob, so the fastest farm is whichever costume you can mow down fastest — usually the Undergrounds slimes.
- **Figurines are 1%** each and also appear as rare Luxury Present rolls (below), so do not despair if you are missing one after a couple of hours.

## Festive Workbench recipes

Buy the bench from the merchant for 5 Cookies & Milk, place it, and craft the set from Snowball, Wood and common early materials.

| Result | Materials |
|---|---|
| Snow Block | 2 Snowball |
| Snowman | 3 Snowball + 2 Wood + 1 Carrock |
| Snowball Pyramid | 5 Snowball + 1 Torch |
| Candle | 1 Torch + 1 Slime |
| Decorated Winter Tree | 10 Wood + 1 Crystal Star |
| Festive Fireplace | 4 Tin Bar + 8 Wood |
| Festive Table | 8 Wood |
| Festive Stool | 2 Wood |
| Festive Wall Lights | 1 Copper Bar |
| Paintable Festive Wall Lights | 1 Copper Bar |
| Red / Green / Blue / Yellow / Purple Firework Rocket | 1 Bomb Pepper + 1 Slime each |
| The BIG | 10 Bomb Pepper + 10 Slime |

The **Decorated Winter Tree** needs a **Crystal Star**, which itself only comes from presents (6.58% in a Winter Present, 8.33% in a Luxury one) — so the centerpiece of a festive base requires a few opened presents before you can craft it.

## FAQ

**When does the Christmas event run?** Roughly Dec 14 to Jan 4 — the longest window of any seasonal event, spanning the new year. Outside those dates you can force it with the season override (Settings → Gameplay, or `-season Christmas` on a dedicated server).

**What is the fastest way to farm Cookies & Milk?** Every costumed mob drops it at a flat 20%, so pick the costume you can kill fastest. The Undergrounds slimes (Snowhead / Carrot) and Santa's Shrooman are the usual choices because they are dense and weak.

**Do I lose the Festive Workbench after Christmas?** No. The physical bench stays placed in your base and its recipes stay available. What disappears is the merchant selling new benches and the mobs dropping Cookies & Milk.

**Why does the Little Helperling sometimes give a Luxury Present?** Because of the rarity of the cooked food you feed it. Uncommon-or-lower food → Winter Present; Rare or higher → Luxury Winter Present. Feed it your best cooked food if you want the Luxury table.

**Is the snow on The Core permanent?** No. The snow cover is a temporary event effect and melts away once Christmas ends, even in worlds where you used the season override.

**Where does the Gingerbread armour come from?** Two places: the Gingerbreadling mob in the Forgotten Ruins (1% per piece) and the Luxury Winter Present table (1.67% per piece). The Gingerbreadman Head only drops from the Starwand Shaman at 2%, or from Luxury Presents.
