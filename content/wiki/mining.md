**Mining** means breaking walls to dig out metal ore and other materials as you explore. Early on you chew through dirt, stone and ore veins by hand, but the real bottleneck is damage thresholds: every wall type absorbs a flat chunk of your mining power before your pickaxe starts actually chipping it. Once Drills unlock later, giant Ore Boulders become a far more efficient supply of metal — but until then, knowing which pickaxe to craft next is the single biggest speed-up available.

## How wall damage actually works

Each strike first loses a flat amount to the wall's damage reduction; only whatever remains goes toward the wall's health. That means a weak pickaxe can ping uselessly against rock that a better tool bites through instantly.

- If your mining stat is too low, the wall makes a dull gong-like noise and knocks you back on the first hit.
- Bare-handed punches ignore this flat deduction entirely, so you can always punch through any breakable wall eventually — it is just slow.
- To earn mining experience from a wall, your mining stat must exceed that wall's reduction by 12 or more.
- Walls heal themselves after going five seconds without a hit, so pausing mid-tunnel wastes progress.

## Ore Boulders and Drills

Once you reach mid-game, the 2×2 Ore Boulders scattered around biomes become your main metal source.

- They are immune to pickaxes and fists; only a Drill powered by an electricity generator or lever can extract them.
- Every boulder yields a fixed 1800 ore of its metal type, and fully draining one takes hours even with eight drills running.
- Drills keep working across the entire world regardless of how far away you wander, so set them up and forget them.
- Boulders spawn mostly by world generation, with the highest density in their matching biome; spawn odds range from frequent (about 1 in 333 tiles) down to very rare (1 in 10,000 or worse).

## Early progression tips

- **Craft the next workbench-tier pickaxe as soon as you can.** A 39-damage Copper pickaxe beats a 15-damage Wood one by more than double and is the biggest early jump.
- **Check the gong.** If you hear that dull knockback sound on a wall, your pickaxe is outclassed — go craft an upgrade rather than wasting swings.
- **Punch weak walls for skill XP.** Deliberately boxing yourself in and punching dirt levels Mining without consuming tool durability.
- **Don't leave walls half-broken.** The five-second self-heal means sustained tunnelling beats stopping and starting.
- **Set up drills early.** Place them on boulders as you explore; the passive ore income pays for itself long before you return.
- **Save explosives for truly hard rock.** Some late walls are impractical to swing through, and blasts or drills are the intended answer.
