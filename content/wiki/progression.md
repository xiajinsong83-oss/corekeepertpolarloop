Core Keeper does not let you wander freely. Progress is gated in two locked ways: **bosses act as doors to the next region**, and **The Great Wall is a hard wall until the inner-boss chain is done**. This page lays out the intended route from The Core to the far endgame, so you know exactly which boss unlocks which area — and which travel tech to craft to keep up.

## The core progression chain

Each main boss drops a required progression item and opens the next tier of gear and the next stretch of map.

| Step | Boss / gate | Region it unlocks | Required drop (per local data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Glurch the Abominous Mass | Dirt ring basics; craft the Hive Mother Scanner | Glurch Eye |
| 2 | Ghorm the Devourer | The inner ring; rails era; Ghorm's Boss Statue | Ghorm's Horn |
| 3 | Hive Mother | The Larva Hive loot room; inner-boss chain complete | Hive Mother Cell |
| — | **The Great Wall breach** | Outer biomes open up | Triggered by completing the inner-boss line via The Core |
| 4 | Azeos the Sky Titan | Azeos' Wilderness (deep) | Azeos' Claw |
| 5 | Omoroth the Sea Titan | The Sunken Sea (deep) | Omoroth's Eye |
| 6 | Ra-Akar the Sand Titan | The Desert of Beginnings (deep) | Ra-Akar's Wing |
| → | Shimmering Frontier → The Passage → Breaker's Reach | Endgame ore tiers (Solarite, Pandorium, Relucite) | — |

The optional boss **Malugaz the Corrupted** lives in the Forgotten Ruins and can be fought any time you reach the stone biome — it is not on the critical path, and it has no scanner.

## How the Great Wall gate works

The wall (the ~450–520 tile ring) cannot be mined, bombed or broken by any tool, at any tier. It comes down only when **The Core** removes it after you clear the three inner bosses — Glurch, Ghorm and Hive Mother. If you reach the wall and it still stands, the game is telling you there is inner-boss work left. Read the full mechanics on [The Great Wall](/wiki/great-wall/).

## The traversal tech ladder

Movement tools unlock in lockstep with how far you can dig, which is half the progression experience:

| Tier | Tool | Unlocked at | What it unlocks for exploration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | Wood Bridges, pickaxe, running/mining skills | By hand / skill levels | Crossing chasms in the inner biomes |
| Tin | Minecarts + Rails | Tin Workbench | A fast shuttle line from The Core to boss arenas |
| Early-mid | Recall Idol | Ghorm's Boss Statue (rare, pricey) | Emergency teleport back to The Core |
| Mid | Boats | Scarlet Workbench | Crossing the water-heavy Sunken Sea |
| Outer | Portals | Octarine Workbench (20-minute charge) | Fast long-distance links between outer biomes |
| Desert | Go-Karts | Go-Kart Workbench | Fast across open Desert (limited visibility, no attacking) |

## Practical route tips

- **Glurch and Ghorm are nearly impossible to miss** when you first strike outward from The Core. Hive Mother sits deeper in the Clay Caves — craft the **Hive Mother Scanner** at Glurch's statue rather than digging blind.
- **Outer-biome bosses** are found with scanners crafted at the Spirit Merchant, or by sweeping the fixed-distance arcs (see [Map Structure](/wiki/world-map/)).
- **Prepare before the breach.** The trip from The Core to the wall is long; lay rails first, and bring boats for the Sunken Sea and portals once you are deep enough.
- **After the wall**, the order of the three outer biomes is up to you, but their ore tiers (Scarlet → Octarine → Galaxite) roughly guide the gear ladder — see the ore table on [Biomes](/wiki/biomes/).

## FAQ

**Can I skip an inner boss and push past the wall?** No. The Great Wall is unbreakable until The Core removes it after the inner-boss chain (Glurch → Ghorm → Hive Mother) is complete.

**Which outer boss should I do first?** The three outer biomes open at once, so choose by gear. Their ores tier up Scarlet (Wilderness) → Octarine (Sunken Sea) → Galaxite (Desert), so follow the ore ladder rather than fighting out of tier.

**Is Malugaz required for progression?** No. It is an optional boss in the Forgotten Ruins. It has no scanner, so it is a side detour rather than a gate.

**When do portals become worth it?** They need 20 minutes to charge and are expensive to craft. They only pay off well into Sunken Sea exploration, after you have mined plenty of Octarine.
