**Crafting** is how you turn raw ore, wood and drops into tools, armour, furniture and automation parts. Almost everything is made at a station you place in your base, although a handful of items can be crafted straight from your inventory. Stations form a long progression ladder: every new bench automatically carries over every recipe from the tier below, so you never have to rebuild an old recipe just because you unlocked a newer one.

## How crafting experience is awarded

You gain crafting XP per *crafting action*, not per item produced. When a single craft spits out two wooden bridges, you only earn one action's worth of XP. The exception is the bulk-craft shortcut: holding **CTRL** and requesting ten items at once is treated as ten separate crafts, so you get ten times the XP.

Each crafting skill level also grants a small defensive bonus — **+0.5% armour per level**, stacking up to +50% at level 100. That passive is why min-maxers keep crafting even after their gear is finished.

### Crafting skill perks

Unlocked points on the skill tree target specific station types rather than generic crafting:

| Perk | Effect (range per level) |
|---|---|
| Base builder | +6–30% chance to gain an extra wall, floor, bridge or fence |
| The right tools in the right hands | +4–20% chance tools/weapons lose no durability on use |
| High quality equipment | +4–20% chance armour loses no durability when you take a hit |
| Alchemist | +4–20% chance to gain an extra item at the Alchemy Table or Distillery |
| Industry specialist | +1–5 extra items when crafting rails, wires or conveyor belts |
| Blacksmith | Anvil recipes and repairs cost 5–25% less material |
| Jewelry crafter | +10–50% chance a jewelry craft comes out as a polished variant |
| Unbreakable | Specialised durability-preservation effect for reinforced gear |

## The workbench ladder

The main bench is the spine of your base. Each tier unlocks a new set of recipes and automatically inherits every recipe from the tiers below it — you never need to place an old Copper Workbench again once you have a Tin one standing.

| Tier | Bench | Unlocked by | Key things it crafts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Basic Workbench | Crafted by hand from wood | Wooden Sword, Wooden Hoe, Watering Can, Furnace, Salvage and Repair Station |
| 1 | Copper Workbench | 8 Wood + 6 Copper Bar | Copper Pickaxe, Railway Forge, Electronics Table |
| 2 | Tin Workbench | 10 Wood + 6 Copper Bar + 10 Tin Bar | Tin tools, Fishing Workbench, Alchemy Table, Automation Table, Loom |
| 3 | Iron Workbench | 10 Plank + 8 Iron Bar | Smelter Kiln, Iron Anvil, Jewelry Workbench, Cartography Table |
| 4 | Scarlet Workbench | 10 Tin Bar + 15 Iron Bar + 20 Scarlet Bar | Scarlet tools, Upgrade Station, Laboratory Table, Boat Workbench |
| 5 | Octarine Workbench | 50 Coral Wood Plank + 20 Scarlet Bar + 30 Octarine Bar | Octarine tools, Portal, Fury Forge, Advanced Automation Table |
| 6 | Galaxite Workbench | 70 Coral Wood Plank + Octarine Bar + 40 Galaxite Bar | Go-Kart Workbench, Galaxite-tier building parts |
| 7 | Solarite Workbench | 70 Solarite Bar | Solarite tools, Gleam Wood building set (inherits every prior bench) |

## The anvil ladder

Anvils are the armour-and-weapon counterpart to workbenches. They follow the same "new tier inherits old" rule, but each anvil is crafted at the corresponding workbench rather than by hand.

| Anvil | Crafted at | Signature gear |
|---|---|---|
| Copper Anvil | Copper Workbench | Copper Helm |
| Tin Anvil | Tin Workbench | Bronze armour, Tin Sword / Axe / Dagger, Slingshot |
| Iron Anvil | Iron Workbench | Iron armour, Iron Shield, Iron Sword |
| Scarlet Anvil | Scarlet Workbench | Scarlet armour and weapons |
| Octarine Anvil | Octarine Workbench | Octarine armour, Octarine Shield |
| Galaxite Anvil | Galaxite Workbench | Galaxite armour, Galaxite Dagger |
| Solarite Anvil | Solarite Workbench | Solarite-tier gear (inherits every prior anvil) |

## Specialised stations by function

Beyond the two ladders, a ring of purpose-built benches handles specific jobs. Most are unlocked partway up the workbench ladder and never replace your main bench:

| Group | Stations | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Woodworking | Carpenter's Table, Table Saw, Loom | Furniture, mannequins, planks, fibre cloth |
| Magic & potions | Alchemy Table, Laboratory Table, Distillery Table | Arcane potions, brewed drinks, advanced concoctions |
| Jewellery | Jewelry Workbench, Proficient Jewelry Workbench | Rings, necklaces, amulets and polished variants |
| Electronics & automation | Electronics Table, Automation Table, Advanced Automation Table | Wires, sensors, conveyor belts, drills, robot arms, seed extractors, shredders |
| Transport | Railway Forge, Boat Workbench, Go-Kart Workbench | Rails, boats, speeders, go-karts |
| Fishing & farming | Fishing Workbench, Livestock Workbench, Egg Incubator | Lures, fishing nets, livestock gear, incubated eggs |
| Building & decoration | Glass Workbench, Painter's Table, Metalcrafter's Table, Key Casting Table | Glass items, paint brushes, warning signs, dungeon keys |
| Music | Music Workbench | Harps, drumkits and other instruments |
| Endgame | Relucite Smithing Table | Void-Fused armour and other late-game smithing |

## Processing stations (no crafting XP)

Furnaces and forges turn raw ore into bars but do not award crafting experience and do not sit on the workbench ladder. They are "input/output" machines: drop ore in, collect bars later.

| Station | Outputs |
|---|---|
| Furnace | Copper Bar, Tin Bar, Iron Bar from their ores |
| Smelter Kiln | Gold Bar, Scarlet Bar, Octarine Bar from their ores |
| Fury Forge | Relucite Bar and other late-game smelting |

Statues and shrines sit in their own bucket — they summon bosses or grant permanent buffs rather than producing craftable items, and are unlocked by exploring rather than by the workbench ladder.

## FAQ

**Do I need to keep old workbenches after building a newer one?** No. Every bench from Copper upward inherits the full recipe list of the tier below, so you can demolish the old bench and free up floor space. The Basic Workbench is the only exception — it is craftable by hand and has no prerequisites, but any later bench also covers its recipes.

**Why did crafting stop giving me XP?** Processing stations such as the Furnace and Smelter Kiln never award crafting XP. If you are only smelting ore, the skill bar will not move; use a workbench or anvil to level up.

**Does bulk crafting with CTRL save materials?** It saves actions and time, but the material cost is multiplied exactly — ten crafts cost ten times the ingredients. You only gain ten times the XP in one go.

**Which crafting perk is worth picking up first?** Blacksmith pays for itself early because anvils and repairs are the most material-heavy recipes in the game. Base builder is a strong second if you enjoy building, since walls, floors, bridges and fences are crafted in bulk.

**Where do seasonal-only recipes come from?** Valentine, Easter, Christmas (Festive) and Halloween (Samhain) each add their own temporary workbench that only exists during the event window. See [Seasonal Workbenches](/wiki/seasonal-workbenches/) for the full list.
