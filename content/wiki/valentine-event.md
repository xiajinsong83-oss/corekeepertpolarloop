The **Valentine Seasonal Event** is the shortest celebration on the Core Keeper calendar, running only **Feb 14 – Feb 17** — four days. Its currency works differently from every other event: instead of farming mob drops, you pick up **Love Letters** scattered on the ground around the world, then spend them at the [Seasonal Merchant](/wiki/seasonal-merchant/) on a heart-themed crafting table, a storage chest and openable gifts.

Because the window is so tight, this page focuses on the exact spend plan and what is inside a Valentine's Gift, so you can clear the merchant on day one. For the full yearly calendar see [Seasonal Events](/wiki/seasonal/), and for how the Valentine Crafting Table fits the event-bench family see [Seasonal Workbenches](/wiki/seasonal-workbenches/).

## How the loop works

1. Wait for the Feb 14 window — or flip the season override (Settings → Gameplay, or `-season Valentine` on a dedicated server).
2. Hang a **Seasonal Calendar** in a suitable room so the [Seasonal Merchant](/wiki/seasonal-merchant/) moves in.
3. **Pick up Love Letters scattered on the ground.** They are not mob drops — walk around familiar biomes and grab them as you explore.
4. Spend Love Letters on the merchant, starting with the Valentine Crafting Table.

## Seasonal Merchant stock

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Valentine Crafting Table | 10 Love Letters | Unlocks the Valentine furniture set |
| Valentine's Chest | 5 Love Letters | Storage with 18 slots |
| Valentine's Gift | 1 Love Letter | Openable container with a random Valentine item |

The obvious budget target is **15 Love Letters** for the bench plus the chest, then whatever spare letters you want to blow on one-letter Gifts.

## Valentine Crafting Table recipes

| Result | Materials |
|---|---|
| Valentine's Candles | 1 Torch + 1 Slime |
| Wall Hearts | 2 Fiber + 1 Heart Berry |
| Heart Painting | 5 Wood + 1 Coral Wood + 2 Fiber |
| Heart-shaped Rug | 10 Fiber + 1 Rug + 1 Heart Berry |
| Caveling Brute Plushie | 1 Caveling Brute Figurine + 10 Fiber |

The **Caveling Brute Plushie** is the craft that asks the most of you: it consumes a real boss figurine (one you would otherwise display), so decide whether the cuddly version belongs in your base before converting the collectible.

## FAQ

**Why is Valentine so easy to miss?** The window is only four days, Feb 14–17 — by far the shortest of any seasonal event. Casual players often blink and it is gone.

**Do Love Letters drop from enemies?** No. Unlike Christmas Cookies & Milk, Love Letters are scattered pickups on the ground. Walk around and sweep them up; killing mobs will not speed you up.

**Can I run Valentine off-season?** Yes. Use the in-game override (Settings → Gameplay → Override season → Valentine) or launch a dedicated server with `-season Valentine`. The merchant will move in once your Calendar is hung.

**What should I buy first?** The Valentine Crafting Table (10 letters) — it unlocks the craftable furniture set. Then the Valentine's Chest (5 letters). Only then spend spare letters on Gifts.

**Do the Necklace and Ring have set bonuses?** The source page lists them as Gift drops but does not document their stats or set effects — treat them as **数据待核** until verified in-game.

**Is the Valentine's Chest worth it?** At 5 letters for 18 slots it is a cheap, themed storage box. If you are short on letters it can wait, since it is purely cosmetic storage.
