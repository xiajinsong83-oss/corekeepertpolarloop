Core Keeper Fishing Filter — Every Fish by Water Type & Catch Chance

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Every fish in Core Keeper, filterable by water type and sortable by catch chance. This fishing filter combines the full fish compendium with interactive controls — tick the biomes you’re fishing in, search for a specific fish, and sort to plan your collection or your next cooking run.

Browse every fish by water type — tick the biomes you fish in, search by name, and sort by catch chance to plan your collection.

Fishing basics

  • Water type decides the fish — each biome’s water holds its own fish table (Dirt Biome, Passage, Sunken Sea, Desert, Crystal…).
  • Catch chance is per fish per water — the listed chance is the relative chance within that water’s pool.
  • Bait helps — lures like the Sweet Lure attract specific fish; check the Fishing Guide for bait recipes.
  • Fish are cooking ingredients — most fish cook into dishes with food or buff effects, so a full compendium doubles as a recipe planner.

How to complete the compendium

  1. Fish every water type — use the water buttons to see what you’re missing per biome.
  2. Sort by catch chance — rare fish are the low-percentage entries; plan to grind those.
  3. Pair with cooking — the fish you catch unlock new dishes at the Cooking Pot.

FAQ

Where do I find rare fish like the Elder Dragonfish? In the Sunken Sea / Crystal waters — use the water filter to see the full table and which biome to visit.

Do fish sell for good money? Some do, but most players use them for cooking — check individual recipes in the Cooking Guide.

Does bait change catch chances? Yes — specific lures target specific fish; the Sweet Lure attracts sweet-named fish like the Heart Berry-lovers.
