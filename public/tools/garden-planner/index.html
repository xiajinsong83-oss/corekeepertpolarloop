How growth works

A watered seed takes exactly 10 minutes: 2 minutes to sprout, 4 to reach pre-harvest, and 4 more to become harvestable. Every plant has a base 3% chance to grow golden, which harvests rarer crops used in strong recipes.

Ground requirements

Most crops grow on any soil, but several are picky: Carrock needs Stone Block, Puffungi needs Mold Block, beach crops (Coral, Pewpaya, Pinegrapple) need Beach Block, and Sunrice / Lunacorn need Crystal Block. Grumpkin only appears during the Creepy Costume Party event.

FAQ

Can I get double harvests? Yes — stack the Farmer’s Hat, Petal Rings, Eostre Necklace and a Kelp Dumpling; full level-19 gear reaches 182.4% harvest chance.

Does harvesting give XP? Only manual harvests of fully grown plants award Gardening experience — wood doesn’t count.
