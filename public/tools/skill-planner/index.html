How Core Keeper skills work

Every skill awards experience passively from its activity — digging for Mining, enemy hits for Melee/Range/Magic/Summoning, kills for Vitality, harvests for Gardening, catches for Fishing, crafts for Crafting, pot pulls for Cooking, and explosions for Explosives. Each level scales a specific bonus; every 5th level also grants a talent point you can spend in that skill’s tree.

FAQ

Which skill should I level first? Mining is the universal opener — faster digging shortens every early loop. Vitality is a close second for survival.

What is the max level? Every skill caps at level 100, worth 20 skill points each (100 points if you max all 10 skill-point skills).

Do points reset? Yes — spending 200 Cave Delicacies lets you reset and reallocate points in a tree at any time.
