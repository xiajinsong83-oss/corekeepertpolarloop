How crop profits work

Every crop in Core Keeper takes exactly 10 minutes from a watered seed to a harvestable plant (2 sprout + 4 grow + 4 mature), so profit scales linearly with how many plots you run. Key mechanics:

  • Sell value — each crop sells for a fixed amount (Heart Berry 1g, Bloat Oat 6g, etc.)
  • Golden crops — a base 3% chance to grow golden versions worth more
  • Extra harvest chance — each Gardening level adds +0.4%, up to +40% at level 100; with the right gear you can double or even triple your harvest
  • Seed recycling — seeds come back on most harvests, so self-grown seeds make seed cost nearly zero over time

FAQ

Is Heart Berry a bad money crop? As a straight sell it’s the weakest, but it’s the best for healing potions and cooking — grow it for utility, not gold.

Do crops grow faster with watering? No — the 10-minute timer applies to watered seeds; unwatered crops simply don’t grow.

What about golden crops? Golden versions sell for more and drop from the 3% golden mutation; the Expert Gardener talent increases that chance.

Why are only 5 crops listed? We only include crops with sell values verified on atma.gg — more are being added as prices get confirmed.
