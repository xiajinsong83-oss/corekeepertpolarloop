Core Keeper Fishing Guide — Best Spots, Bait, the Fishing Workbench and Rare Fish

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Fishing in Core Keeper is more than a side activity: it is a second ore income, a key source of accessories, and the only way to summon Omoroth later. This advanced Core Keeper fishing guide explains how the catch system decides what you pull out, how to set up a fishing corner, and which water to stand in for the rare fish and valuables you actually want.

How the catch system works

Three things decide what you catch:

  • Water type first. Colored water (yellow, moldy, seawater, lava, shimmering, grimy) always uses its own loot table, wherever it is on the map. Normal water only falls back to the local biome table.
  • Fishing power must clear the water. If your fishing power is below the water’s requirement, you hook nothing. Once you clear it, extra power shortens the reeling time. Each fishing skill level gives +1 fishing power (max +100 at level 100).
  • The off-hand slot holds bait. Lures and the Expert Lure-type items are placed in your off-hand slot to tweak bite speed, fish chance and rarity.

A bobber that lands on a fish splash guarantees a fish-only catch and triggers the reeling mini-game. Materials and valuables, by contrast, are pulled out instantly — which makes non-fish pulls the fastest way to grind fishing experience early on.

The Fishing Workbench and your first lures

Once you have a Tin Workbench, craft the Fishing Workbench itself (8 Wood + 5 Tin Bar). It is the station that makes nets and lures:

The practical takeaway: fishing your way to ore beats manual digging once you can reach the right water. Scarlet from Mold water, Octarine from the Sunken Sea and Galaxite from Desert/lava water all come without swinging a pickaxe.

Fishing skill talents worth taking

  • Improved bait — faster bites (the quality-of-life one).
  • Fisherman’s luck — higher chance the hook lands on a fish rather than junk.
  • Angler’s advantage — boosts the bite chance of rarer fish.
  • Chewy bait — lures last longer, which matters once endgame lures are expensive.
  • Power of Omega-3! — damage bonus against bosses after eating fish or fish-cooked food.

FAQ

Why do I catch nothing when I fish? Your fishing power must exceed the water’s required power. Each fishing skill level adds +1 power, up to +100 at level 100. Deeper-endgame water (lava, shimmering, grimy) needs a higher level.

How do I get the Fishing Workbench? Craft it at a Tin Workbench with 8 Wood and 5 Tin Bar. It unlocks fishing nets and the four lure types.

Can I fish for ore instead of mining? Yes — Forgotten Ruins water gives Iron Ore (20.59%) and Gold Ore, Mold water gives Scarlet Ore (22.43%), Sunken Sea water gives Octarine Ore, and Desert/lava water gives Galaxite Ore.
