Core Keeper NPC and Villager Guide — Room Requirements, Merchants and Move-in Rules

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Core Keeper’s towns don’t grow by themselves: every settled resident moves in because you built the right room, put the right item in it, and walked away long enough for them to notice. This NPC guide covers the verified room system, how merchants and seasonal visitors work, and where to find trader cells in the wild.

What counts as a valid move-in room

The game only registers a room for NPC purposes when all four conditions hold at the same time:

ConditionRequirementCommon failure
SizeEnclosed area between 2×2 and 8×8 tiles9×9 hallways and 1×3 corridors silently don’t count
AccessA door on the enclosureOpen doorways without a door block don’t register
FurnitureA bed insideBed must be placed, not just carried in inventory
Welcome itemThe specific required item for that NPC, placed in the roomWrong item = the NPC never moves in

Then the final trigger: no players within roughly 30 tiles of the room for about a minute. Standing inside checking whether it worked is the classic reason nothing happens — walk to the Core and wait.

How move-in actually unfolds

  1. You clear a pocket of the world, wall it in, add door + bed.
  2. You place the NPC’s welcome item on the floor (each recruitable resident wants a different one — see table below).
  3. You leave the area for about a minute.
  4. The room registers, the NPC moves in, and the door becomes “their” door.

Practically: build your NPC row in a quiet corner of the base, not next to your minecart loop — foot traffic delays move-in, and you’ll think the room is broken.

Trader residents and seasonal visitors

Verified trader mechanics in the game today:

NPC / visitorHow they arriveWhat they do
Merchant cells (wild traders)Hand-designed scenes found in the world — notably islands inside the Forlorn Metropolis in the Sunken SeaTraders you meet on location; scene hunting is how you find them
Seasonal MerchantReal-world calendar event — hang a Calendar in a suitable room and they appear during the event windowSells event items and seasonal workbenches for event currency
Settled trade NPCsMove in via the room system aboveTrade from your base once settled

Two seasonal rules worth remembering:

  • The merchant lives in your world save — in multiplayer that means the host’s world (or the dedicated server’s); everyone online trades with the same visitor.
  • Event currency and event-bench purchases are per player: each member of a co-op world needs their own currency to buy their own bench, even though everyone can craft from the same placed bench.

FAQ

Why is my NPC not moving into the room? Check the four gates: room size 2×2 to 8×8, a door plus a bed, the specific required item placed inside, and no players standing within ~30 tiles for about a minute.

Where do merchants live in Core Keeper? Merchant cells are hand-built scenes you find in the wild (notably islands of the Forlorn Metropolis in the Sunken Sea); the seasonal merchant visits on a real-world calendar schedule once you hang up a Calendar.

Do NPCs stay in my world forever? Once settled, an NPC belongs to that world save — in multiplayer the host’s world (or the dedicated server’s) is where everyone trades with them.
