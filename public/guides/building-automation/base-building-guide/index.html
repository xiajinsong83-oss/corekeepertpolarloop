Core Keeper Base Building Guide — Room Rules, NPC Housing and Automation

Last updated: 2026-10-18

A good Core Keeper base is more than a pile of chests. This base building guide covers the room system, NPC move-in rules, bed mechanics and the automation basics that keep your underground hub running while you explore.

Room rules that actually matter

Rooms are the core building block of a Core Keeper base. The game recognizes a room when:

  • The enclosed area is between 2×2 and 8×8 tiles (smaller or larger rooms don’t register)
  • It has a door and a bed
  • It contains the required item for the NPC you want (each NPC has a specific item)
  • No players are within 30 tiles of the room for roughly a minute

If any condition fails, the room won’t count — and NPCs won’t move in.

Respawn points: use beds strategically

Your spawn point is the Core or your most recently slept-in bed. Two practical uses:

  1. Boss prep: place a bed near a boss arena, sleep briefly, and you’ll respawn at that bed instead of running back from the Core.
  2. Exploration outposts: mini-bases with a bed, a few chests and a workbench let you operate far from home without long corpse runs.

Base location checklist

  • Near the Core: the Core is your main fast-travel anchor and power source — build the first hub within ~30–60 tiles.
  • Flat, clear ground: dirt walls are quick to flatten with a shovel.
  • Close to a room you’ll use early: a lake (water access) and a farm patch reduce early travel.

Automation basics

  • Smelting arrays: build several Furnaces side by side; feed them with chests of ore and they process in parallel while you’re away.
  • Crafting stations as rooms: workbenches, furnaces and cooking pots work best when each is its own registered room with a door.
  • Farming: hoe the ground, plant seeds, water regularly — crops restore food and fuel cooking. Gardening skill shortens grow times as it levels.
  • Later-game conveyor belts: belts and automation machines unlock in later tiers; the Base Layout Planner helps you sketch conveyor layouts before you commit resources.

FAQ

Why won’t an NPC move into my room? Check the three conditions: size between 2×2 and 8×8, a door + bed, the NPC’s required item, and nobody within 30 tiles for about a minute.

Can rooms be bigger than 8×8? They can exist, but they won’t register as a valid room for NPC purposes — split large halls into separate rooms.

How do I change my spawn point? Sleep in a bed. Your respawn switches to that bed until you sleep somewhere else.
