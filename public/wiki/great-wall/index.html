Core Keeper The Great Wall — The Outer Biome Gate

Last updated: 2026-10-18

The Great Wall is an unbreakable, undulating ring of mechanical-looking blocks that separates the three inner biomes from the outer world. It is not a natural formation you can dig through — every tool, weapon and explosive bounces off it. It exists purely as a progression gate: until The Core lets you take it down, the Azeos’ Wilderness, the Sunken Sea and the Desert of Beginnings are off-limits no matter how far you walk.

Where the wall sits on your map

The wall occupies the band roughly 450 to 520 tiles from The Core, sitting between the inner biomes (which end at about 450 tiles) and the start of the outer biomes (which begin around 520 tiles). It wraps all the way around:

ZoneDistance from CoreRelationship to the wall
Undergrounds (dirt)0 – ~140Inside the wall
Clay Caves + Forgotten Ruins~150 – 450Inside the wall
The Great Wall~450 – 520The gate itself
Outer biomes~520 +Outside the wall

The border is not a perfect circle. It uses a wavy, convolution-generated edge that rises and falls like a coastline, which is why the wall can feel closer on one side of your map than on the opposite side. Its angular position is fixed for every world, but the undulation pattern is part of the deterministic terrain that a given seed reproduces.

What it looks like — and what it is not

Visually the wall is a dark, tiled structure with a mechanical finish, often lined with small torch posts that light up the gap. This is deliberate: it is easy to confuse with the Obsidian Blocks that fill un-generated terrain, but the two are not the same.

  • Obsidian Blocks are the natural, dark rock used as default filler in chunks that have not been rendered yet. They appear across all biomes and, with the right tools, are breakable.
  • The Great Wall is a specific hand-placed structure. Its tiles have a distinct mechanical texture and are impervious to every tool, weapon and bomb in the game.

If you hit a dark wall that breaks, it was Obsidian filler. If you hit a dark wall that does not, and you are roughly 450–520 tiles out, you have found The Great Wall.

How to actually get past

You cannot mine the wall down. The intended path is progression through The Core:

  1. Clear the three inner bosses — Glurch the Abominous Mass, Ghorm the Devourer, and Hive Mother.
  2. Once the inner-boss line is complete, The Core becomes the mechanism that takes the wall down. The wall does not disappear tile-by-tile from mining; it is removed by the game in response to that progression.
  3. After the breach is open, you are free to walk, bridge or mine out into the outer biomes.

In practice, this means you should not waste pickaxe durability scratching at the wall when you first reach it. If the wall is still standing, the game is telling you that you have inner-boss work left to do.

Practical tips

  • Scout the wall before breaching. Walk along its inner edge to spot where the outer-biome angles are. A seed-finder tool can tell you which direction Azeos’ Wilderness, the Sunken Sea and the Desert of Beginnings sit, so you know which gap to aim for once the wall is down.
  • Bring rails or a recall idol. The trip from The Core to the wall is long even after you tunnel out. Setting up a rail line before the breach makes post-wall exploration much faster.
  • Do not confuse the wall with chasms. Chasms and gorges are generated by Voronoi noise and can be bridged or tunnelled around. The wall is the single hard ring; everything else in the outer band is normal terrain.
  • The wall is not a mob wall. It does not spawn enemies on its own. The difficulty spike on the other side comes from the outer biomes’ own mobs and bosses, not from the wall itself.

FAQ

Can I dig through The Great Wall with a late-game pickaxe? No. Every tool tier — including Solarite and Relucite — bounces off the wall. The wall is removed only by progressing through The Core after the three inner bosses are defeated.

Does the wall have a gap I can walk through? There is no pre-built gate. The undulating border is continuous around the inner biomes; you cross it after the game removes it for you.

Is The Great Wall the same as Obsidian Blocks? No. Obsidian Blocks are terrain filler that can be mined, especially with stronger pickaxes. The Great Wall is a hand-placed mechanical structure with a distinct texture and is unbreakable.

Can I see where the wall is on the map before reaching it? The wall appears on the in-game map once the surrounding chunks have been generated. If you have not explored that far, the band will not render yet — use a seed-finder tool to preview the ring before you walk out.

Does the wall exist in hard mode or multiplayer? Yes. It is part of world generation, not a difficulty modifier, and every world — hard mode, normal, dedicated server — has the same 450–520 tile ring.
