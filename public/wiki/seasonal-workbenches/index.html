Core Keeper Seasonal Workbenches — Event-Only Crafting Stations

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Each recurring celebration adds its own event-only workbench to the game. These benches are not part of the normal workbench ladder — they do not inherit recipes from the Copper/Tin/Iron tier, and they are not permanent unlocks. They exist only during their event window, are bought from the Seasonal Merchant, and vanish from the merchant’s stock when the celebration ends. Anything you have already crafted, however, stays in your storage forever.

The four event workbenches

BenchEventWindowUnlocks
Valentine Crafting TableValentine Seasonal EventFeb 14 – Feb 17Heart-themed décor and Valentine craftables
Easter WorkbenchThe Great Egg Hunt (Easter)Apr 5 – Apr 16Easter eggs, spring décor and hunt-themed items
Festive WorkbenchChristmas Seasonal EventDec 14 – Jan 4Full festive building set, holiday food, presents, fireworks
Samhain WorkbenchCreepy Costume Party (Halloween)Oct 25 – Nov 7Spooky furniture, eerie table and stool, Halloween décor

A few notes on each:

  • Valentine Crafting Table is the shortest-lived bench of all — its window is only four days. If you want the Valentine set, log in on February 14 and buy the bench immediately.
  • Easter Workbench ties into the egg hunt: it lets you craft with the coloured eggs you find during the event, including the large golden egg pieces.
  • Festive Workbench is the richest of the four. It covers snow blocks, a Festive Workbench itself, a Gingerbread House Chest, a fireplace, stool, table, snow blocks and a Decorated Winter Tree, plus holiday food and wrapped presents.
  • Samhain Workbench is the Halloween counterpart: it crafts the Eerie Stool, Eerie Table and other spooky pieces that match the Pumpkin Head cosmetic and boss figurines.

How to obtain one

Every event bench follows the same loop:

  1. Wait for the corresponding event window to open (or set the season override manually).
  2. Summon the Seasonal Merchant with a Seasonal Calendar hung in a suitable room.
  3. Farm event currency from themed mobs — Cookies & Milk for Christmas, event drops for the others.
  4. Buy the workbench from the merchant. The Christmas Festive Workbench, for example, costs 5 Cookies & Milk.
  5. Place the bench in your base and craft the event set at your leisure.

Little Helperling — the Christmas companion

Technically not a workbench, but it is grouped with the Christmas crafting stations on the wiki: the Little Helperling is a small companion you feed cooked food during Christmas. In return it crafts presents for you automatically. It is part of the same event window as the Festive Workbench and disappears when Christmas ends.

What happens to the bench after the event

  • The bench itself stays placed in your base. You do not lose the physical station when the calendar rolls over.
  • Recipes stay available. Even after the event ends, the bench you already built still shows its full recipe list. You can keep crafting event décor from stored materials all year.
  • The merchant stops selling it. If you missed the window and never bought the bench, you have to wait until next year — or use the season override to bring the merchant back.
  • Event currency drops stop. Themed mobs revert to normal once the event ends, so Cookies & Milk and similar drops stop accumulating.

This is why the standard advice is: buy the bench on day one of the event. It costs only a few currency units, and without it you cannot craft any of the event set no matter how much décor you have seen.

Practical tips

  • Hang the Calendar early. Have a Seasonal Crafted at the Carpenter’s Table and hung on a wall before the event starts, so the merchant moves in the moment the calendar flips.
  • Craft a storage chest per event. Christmas items, Easter eggs and Halloween décor pile up fast. Label the chest so next year’s additions do not mix with the old set.
  • Use the override for catch-up runs. Missed Valentine? Set -season Valentine on a server, or flip the in-game override, buy the bench, craft the set, then set it back.
  • Do not confuse event benches with the permanent ladder. The Festive, Samhain, Easter and Valentine tables are side-content. Your main workbench progression (Copper → Tin → Iron → Scarlet → Octarine → Galaxite → Solarite) is unaffected by whether you own them.

FAQ

Can I craft event items all year if I already bought the bench? Yes. The bench stays placed and its recipes stay available. What you lose after the event ends is the merchant selling new benches and the themed mobs dropping event currency.

Which event bench has the most content? The Christmas Festive Workbench, by a wide margin. It covers building blocks, furniture, food, presents, fireworks and the Little Helperling companion.

Is the Samhain Workbench the same as the Halloween bench? Yes — “Samhain” is the internal / wiki name for the Halloween workbench. The event itself is called the Creepy Costume Party in-game.

Do event benches count toward the crafting skill? Crafting at them awards normal crafting XP like any other station, so they are a legitimate way to grind the crafting skill during the event window.

Can I gift an event bench to a friend? No. The bench is bought from the merchant with event currency and placed by the buyer. Each player in a co-op world needs their own currency and their own purchase, though they can all craft from the same placed bench.
