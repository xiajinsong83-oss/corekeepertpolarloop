Core Keeper Valentine Event — Love Letters, Gifts and the Crafting Table

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Source: Valentine Seasonal Event — Core Keeper Wiki (atma.gg). Prices, recipes and drop rates below are taken directly from that page; the four-day window comes from this site’s verified Seasonal Events calendar.

The Valentine Seasonal Event is the shortest celebration on the Core Keeper calendar, running only Feb 14 – Feb 17 — four days. Its currency works differently from every other event: instead of farming mob drops, you pick up Love Letters scattered on the ground around the world, then spend them at the Seasonal Merchant on a heart-themed crafting table, a storage chest and openable gifts.

Because the window is so tight, this page focuses on the exact spend plan and what is inside a Valentine’s Gift, so you can clear the merchant on day one. For the full yearly calendar see Seasonal Events, and for how the Valentine Crafting Table fits the event-bench family see Seasonal Workbenches.

How the loop works

  1. Wait for the Feb 14 window — or flip the season override (Settings → Gameplay, or -season Valentine on a dedicated server).
  2. Hang a Seasonal Calendar in a suitable room so the Seasonal Merchant moves in.
  3. Pick up Love Letters scattered on the ground. They are not mob drops — walk around familiar biomes and grab them as you explore.
  4. Spend Love Letters on the merchant, starting with the Valentine Crafting Table.

The obvious budget target is 15 Love Letters for the bench plus the chest, then whatever spare letters you want to blow on one-letter Gifts.

FAQ

Why is Valentine so easy to miss? The window is only four days, Feb 14–17 — by far the shortest of any seasonal event. Casual players often blink and it is gone.

Do Love Letters drop from enemies? No. Unlike Christmas Cookies & Milk, Love Letters are scattered pickups on the ground. Walk around and sweep them up; killing mobs will not speed you up.

Can I run Valentine off-season? Yes. Use the in-game override (Settings → Gameplay → Override season → Valentine) or launch a dedicated server with -season Valentine. The merchant will move in once your Calendar is hung.

What should I buy first? The Valentine Crafting Table (10 letters) — it unlocks the craftable furniture set. Then the Valentine’s Chest (5 letters). Only then spend spare letters on Gifts.

Do the Necklace and Ring have set bonuses? The source page lists them as Gift drops but does not document their stats or set effects — treat them as 数据待核 until verified in-game.

Is the Valentine’s Chest worth it? At 5 letters for 18 slots it is a cheap, themed storage box. If you are short on letters it can wait, since it is purely cosmetic storage.
