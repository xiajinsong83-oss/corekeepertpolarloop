Core Keeper Seasonal Events

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Seasonal events are short, theme-driven celebrations that transform the underworld for a few weeks every year — pumpkins in autumn, snow and presents in winter, cherry blossoms in spring. During each window, certain enemies and bosses get a visual reskin, the main-menu logo changes to match the theme, and a roster of exclusive food, vanity and building items becomes available. Most of that limited loot is sold by the travelling Seasonal Merchant, so collecting the full set depends on logging in at the right time.

The yearly event calendar

The game activates events automatically based on the real-world date, so you do not need to switch anything on. Each celebration also swaps in a themed logo on the title screen.

A few practical notes on the schedule:

  • Outside these dates nothing is active; the world looks and behaves normally.
  • Christmas runs the longest, spanning roughly three weeks across the new year.
  • Valentine is by far the shortest window, lasting only four days, so it is easy to miss if you play occasionally.
  • Jingle Jam was a one-off charity event held in December 2021 and does not recur.

What changes during each celebration

When an event is live, you will notice three things at once:

  • Enemies and bosses wearing themed costumes that fit the occasion.
  • A recoloured main-menu logo — the orange Halloween variant and the frosty Christmas one are the most recognisable.
  • Event-exclusive stock from the Seasonal Merchant, plus themed décor you can craft yourself.

Halloween (the Creepy Costume Party) is mostly about spooky vanity: a Pumpkin Head cosmetic and a line of themed boss figurines such as the Spookaveling, the Pumpking Caveling and the Haunted Hive Mother trinket. Christmas is by far the largest loot drop, covering comfort food — Cookies & Milk, Hot Chocolate, Hot Punch, Fruit Bread and a full Winter Dinner — wrapped presents in both regular and Luxury Winter Present versions, and a complete festive building set. That set includes a Festive Workbench, a Gingerbread House Chest, a fireplace, stool, table, snow blocks and a Decorated Winter Tree, with the Little Helperling companion rounding out the collection.

Running an event outside its window

If you want Halloween decorations in summer or a white Christmas in July, you do not have to wait for the real calendar:

  • From the title screen, before choosing a world or character, open Settings → Gameplay settings → Override season.
  • Choose the celebration you want, or pick the off option to return to the normal automatic rotation.
  • In multiplayer, the host’s setting — or the dedicated server’s — takes priority; everyone joining follows it.
  • On a dedicated server, pass a launch property such as -season Halloween, -season Christmas, -season Easter, -season Valentine or -season None.

This override is the cleanest way to farm event trophies on demand, whether for screenshots, holiday bases or simply completing your collection.

What this means for players

  • Grab everything before the window closes. Once the date rolls past, event stock leaves the merchant and themed mobs revert to normal. Buy every blueprint and décor piece while it is available.
  • Save currency for the merchant. Event stock is on the event’s schedule, not yours — having coins ready on day one means you are not scrambling on the final evening.
  • Design builds around the décor. The festive furniture, snow blocks and pumpkin cosmetics are build-friendly, so sketch out a holiday base ahead of time and you can furnish it the moment the event unlocks.
  • Use the override for catch-up runs. Missed Valentine? Flip the override, collect the items, then set it back. Server hosts can even lock a season permanently for a themed world.
  • Check news and patch notes. Event dates and item rosters occasionally shift with updates, so glance at the latest patches before clearing out storage expecting a favourite to return.
