Core Keeper Halloween Event — Creepy Costume Party and Samhain

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Source: this page is synthesised from this site’s own verified seasonal notes (Seasonal Events, Seasonal Workbenches, Seasonal Merchant). Note: atma.gg has no dedicated Halloween / Samhain article (the candidate URLs return 404), so exact drop tables and recipes below are intentionally omitted and marked 数据待核 rather than guessed.

The Halloween Seasonal Event — called the Creepy Costume Party in-game, and Samhain in the wiki’s internal naming — runs roughly Oct 25 – Nov 7. It is the autumn counterpart to Christmas: enemies dress up in spooky costumes, the title-screen logo flips to its orange variant, and the Seasonal Merchant opens a small, vanity-focused stock. Where Christmas is a loot explosion, Halloween is mostly about cosplay and spooky décor — a Pumpkin Head cosmetic, a line of boss figurines, and an eerie furniture set.

For the full yearly calendar and the season override, see Seasonal Events; for how the Samhain Workbench compares with the other event benches, see Seasonal Workbenches.

What changes during the Creepy Costume Party

When the event goes live you will notice three things at once:

  • Themed mob costumes. Existing enemies and bosses are reskinned with Halloween outfits, and they drop event-specific loot on top of their normal drops.
  • The orange main-menu logo. The Halloween title-screen variant is one of the most recognisable logo swaps in the game.
  • A new merchant stock. The Seasonal Merchant sells the Pumpkin Head cosmetic, boss figurines and the event workbench.

As with every celebration, the event runs automatically off the real-world date — you do not switch anything on. To run it in summer, use the override: Settings → Gameplay settings → Override season → Halloween, or launch a dedicated server with -season Halloween.

The four figurines — Spookaveling, Pumpking Caveling and the Haunted Hive Mother — are display pieces that nod to early-game bosses, re-skinned for the occasion. They are the centrepiece collectibles of the event.

The Samhain Workbench

The Samhain Workbench is Halloween’s answer to the Christmas Festive Workbench, but much smaller. Bought from the Seasonal Merchant during the event window, it crafts the spooky furniture line — confirmed recipes include the Eerie Stool and Eerie Table, which match the Pumpkin Head cosmetic.

Two rules apply to every event bench, and the Samhain one is no exception:

  • Buy it on day one. If you miss the window and never buy the bench, the merchant stops selling it — you wait until next year or use the override.
  • Keep it once placed. The physical bench stays in your base after Halloween ends and its recipes remain available, so you can keep crafting spooky décor from stored materials all year.

The Samhain Workbench costs event-specific mob-drop currency to buy. The exact currency item and its price are 数据待核 — atma.gg has no dedicated Halloween article, and this site’s existing notes only describe Christmas currency (Cookies & Milk) in detail.

Practical tips

  • Grab the Pumpkin Head early. It is the event’s signature cosmetic and leaves with the merchant on Nov 7.
  • Collect all three figurines in one run. They are merchant stock, not mob drops — buy them all the same day you summon the merchant.
  • Hang the Calendar before Oct 25. Have a Seasonal Calendar (Carpenter’s Table recipe) on a wall ahead of time so the merchant moves in the moment the window opens.
  • Use the override for catch-up. Missed Halloween? Flip the season override, buy the bench and figurines, craft the Eerie furniture, then set the season back.

FAQ

When does the Creepy Costume Party run? Roughly Oct 25 – Nov 7, about two weeks — shorter than Christmas but much longer than Valentine’s four-day window.

Is “Samhain” the same as the Halloween event? Yes. “Creepy Costume Party” is the in-game event name; “Samhain” is the internal/wiki name for the event (and its workbench). They refer to the same celebration.

What is the Pumpkin Head? A Halloween cosmetic helmet sold by the Seasonal Merchant during the event. It pairs with the spooky furniture set from the Samhain Workbench.

Do the Halloween figurines do anything? They are display collectibles — like the boss trophies you get from normal boss fights, reskinned for the occasion. They go on shelves and pedestals in your base.

Can I craft Halloween furniture after the event ends? Yes, provided you bought and placed the Samhain Workbench before the window closed. The bench stays and its recipes stay; only the merchant and the mob currency drops leave.
